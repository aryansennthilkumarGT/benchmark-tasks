# Observable local coupon score

The simulation evaluates quasistatic equilibrium return and does not certify dynamic settling or a timed dwell. This grades a planar mechanical coupon, not a complete RoboCurve assembly. All tolerances/probe forces below are authored test criteria, not designer acceptance limits.

A nominal coupon uses all twelve genuine-size tube elements in the two source contact banks. Release every manipulating tool. Under the designer-reported approximate 10 g moving load, measure the foot's center relative to the fixed leg. Then apply and remove a 1 N in-plane probe upward and in each horizontal direction, leaving the same elements/settings throughout.

Full local credit requires:

- Twelve 6/4×3 mm elements, each in the actual annular contact band; no support prop or substituted rigid filler.
- Own-weight center offset below 0.3 mm.
- Displacement between 0.1 and 1.3 mm for each tested 1 N load.
- Return within 0.1 mm of the initial own-weight equilibrium after every load removal.

Use calipers for stock, direct count/photos while this open-bank coupon is visible, a dial indicator or scaled macro video for displacement, and an ordinary force gauge for probes. Numerical solver convergence/contact-domain checks separately invalidate unreliable simulation results; they are not real-world success flags. A numerical travel boundary is never scored as retention.

Missing one ring or an entire bank may still allow return. The function test alone cannot certify complete assembly. The full closed-device task would need a demonstrated nondestructive inspection method for both banks; this checkpoint does not solve that observability gate.

No sensor voltage or LED is synthesized. The complete task must additionally prove axial retention after closure, correct antirotation pin and electronics/wiring, source-compatible multidirectional travel and real reversible optical output. It must also exclude damaged silicone/print parts and retained support. This rigid-structure/elastic-beam coupon has no calibrated damage model and cannot certify those outcomes.
