## New findings that change the next binding gate

1. **The actual photos distinguish two protections.** `wire-management-types.jpg` clearly shows yellow open helical ribbon wrapping bundles along structure. The two moving-parts photos show orange corrugated tubular sheathing around a geared pivot, with yellow open spiral wrap adjacent on the arm. The guide's paragraph calls the orange object a **split sheath**. Its seam and internal geometry are not measured.
2. **Alt text alone is unsafe subtype evidence.** The moving-parts image alt strings call the orange object “spiral,” but the inspected visual and paragraph establish the corrugated/split example's distinct form. Do not implement a single helical-ribbon model for both. This is a source-description mismatch, not proof that the entire guide or actual robot wiring is invalid.
3. **The actual selected span is not bound.** The guide's yellow-sleeve/arm-to-grabber paragraph supports real robot use. It does not dimension which photo span, actual joint limits, contacts, cut length, wrap pitch, ribbon section or installed anchoring configuration should be used. The geared-pivot orange example must not silently supply these inputs for the yellow helix.
4. **Branch passage is still a proposed challenge, not an established procedure step.** Photos show a real multi-wire harness, but no source-defined branch inventory/breakout geometry or specific wrapping-around-branches sequence has been selected. Do not add a difficult artificial fork or require a branch-passage skill merely to distinguish other cable tasks.

These findings narrow the next source question. They do not establish exact sleeve material/SKU identity from color, dimensional tolerances, opening force or YAM access.
