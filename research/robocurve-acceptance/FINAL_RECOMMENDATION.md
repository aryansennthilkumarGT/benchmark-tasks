## What the acceptance forensics changed

- Native experts, static tests, receipts and genuine model videos prove neither occupational importance nor semantic novelty. Our four final rejections are strong direct evidence of that separation.
- A real manual establishes that a procedure exists, not that it is regular core work for every member of a large occupation. #1472,#1739,#1775 and#1750 repeatedly reject that inference.
- Earlier accepted simplifications are local precedents, not permission to invent equipment or remove its essential interfaces. #1084 was rejected for missing actual hose handling and gasket compression; that does not prove authentic cam-and-groove coupling can never qualify, so it remains demoted rather than categorically banned.
- Useful released physical outcomes and reproducible measurements are central. #1562 calibrated snug; #1576 rewarded actual seal seating; #1495 separated simulated geometry proxies from real bead quality; #1577 restored pressure-dependent piston movement.
- Changing connector controls, component count, entry route or final housekeeping often fails novelty. Current comparisons must include earlier pending submissions and external task mechanics, not just accepted folder names.
