# Fresh idea bank: discovery before screening

This bank excludes the forty historical candidates in CANDIDATES_40.md and the six preserved Cargo/ER/SDS/nailer/shackle/SAC concepts. These are recognizable real-world operations proposed from general equipment knowledge. **UNVERIFIED means the procedure, model applicability, occupational regularity and novelty have not yet been established by reading the target source.** Search targets below are requests for evidence, not invented citations. Initial difficulty is qualitative engineering judgment, not measured runtime. Approximate occupation sizes in Version21 are context only; no arbitrary equipment duty inherits them.

Each entry records all ten discovery fields. Novelty statements are hypotheses to challenge against actual accepted/pending source code. A different tool/brand or elementary motion alone is insufficient. Suspected internal families are called out in the batch audit; they are not assumed to be independent accepted tasks.

## Internal distinction audit

No exact title or capability description is repeated. Distinct words alone do not prove distinct skills: the following families require mechanism-level screening before ranking. Blind riveting versus rivet-nut setting differs in permanent sheet joining versus creation of a threaded insert; wire stripping versus ferrule crimping differs in controlled removal versus forming a conductive termination; sealant application versus gasket seating differs in flow/tool path versus elastic capture. Retain both only if those actual contacts and useful outcomes remain central. Generic screw/connector/clip variations must be consolidated if later research reveals equivalent manipulation and success conditions.

The bank is a discovery inventory, not a claim of100 novel, eligible or feasible submissions. Ranking will not label unsupported concepts STRONG merely to meet a target.
