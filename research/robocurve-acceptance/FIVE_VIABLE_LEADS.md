Read [Elantra p863](https://github.com/Kmberry1989/Elantra/blob/0f7b23efdcf6ecc48919fb781be7e95a1b8f0f06/2013%20Hyundai%20Elantra%20L4-1.8L/pages/863.html) and [p887](https://github.com/Kmberry1989/Elantra/blob/0f7b23efdcf6ecc48919fb781be7e95a1b8f0f06/2013%20Hyundai%20Elantra%20L4-1.8L/pages/887.html), plus actual perimeter/handle/connector diagrams. Provenance is a pinned CHARM reproduction, not authenticated Hyundai-hosted bytes. Motor service explicitly begins by removing trim, so the importance bridge is tighter than “mechanics do cosmetic work.”

The proposed complete handoff removes clip-retained trim and its genuine remaining handle/electrical attachments onto ordinary independent support. Prerequisite screws/quadrant cover may be normally removed in a clearly bounded start; essential clip capture and rear connections remain. No free plate, generic spring-mounted panel or unexplained loose module. The source permits replacing damaged clips: do not invent universal clip reusability or call ordinary replacement damage a fatal task outcome without defining scope.

**Strongest rejection:** an inverse/component variant of #1986, or a one-clip proxy borrowing a whole repair's importance. The positive case must rest on actual tool-mediated release, panel loading and real attached interfaces, not extra clip count. **Next gate:** bind donor/VIN, clip/tool/plug geometry and test a full-size stock-finger/palm path on drawings. Missing that is a planning input, not a rapid-screen rejection.
