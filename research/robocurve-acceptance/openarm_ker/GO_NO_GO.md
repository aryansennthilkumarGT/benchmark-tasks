## Exact HOLD block

The inspected STEP has static product occurrences, not verified rigid-link membership, encoder-joint axes/limits and a complete calibration-ready articulated state registered to all the jig's datums. Its exported configuration must change to mate the inclined pipe seat and J8 sites. Official KER streaming software and follower-arm description repositories were inspected; they did not supply that matched KER definition. A follower URDF scaled by70%, a rigid509-body payload, or poses guessed from an illustration would bypass the central physical problem.

This is the **next single closure item**, not a claim that one new file guarantees GO. The real M2 tool/grasp, normal support and jig material/load response remain downstream work, explicitly unassessed. No missing entry was filled with a convenient dimension/force. Source CAD already establishes useful local geometry, but a standalone pipe/groove coupon would not test the candidate's claimed coupled datum/retention ability or resolve its closest novelty objection.

## Why not GO or PARK

A source-backed **mechanism** coupon is not yet justified as the first implementation investment: the actual configuration/load transfer and central distinction are not established. Therefore no generic spring latch, beam law, welded leader or force-at-depth success model was built. The static CAD diagnostics are not presented as that prototype.

PARK is also unsupported now. Real geometry/procedure/data-collection purpose are available, the required datums exist, and neither genuine full-scale reach/support impossibility nor a definite duplicate has been proved. The native260×525.2×293.993mm export envelope and nominal1.7kg product mass do not by themselves demonstrate a stock-YAM failure or pass. Missing path/grasp data is uncertainty, not measured infeasibility.

## Return criteria

2. Select a real suitable M2 tool and grip/support strategy. Use complete YAM/KER/jig/tool geometry for candidate pose and swept-path tests; do not exempt palms/wrists or silently remove surrounding structure. Include the articulated assembly and release of support.
3. Show that coupled posture retention is essential and materially distinct from #1900 and generic datum seating/fastening. Recheck current official scope before implementation.
4. Then select one bounded datum/body-lock contact unit with actual boundary/load path. Source-bound geometry plus disclosed material uncertainty may justify a reduced model; perfect FEM or a finished native demo is not required. Run meaningful incomplete/unsupported/loose/overloaded controls and keep full-task readiness separate.
