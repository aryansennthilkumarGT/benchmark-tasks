# Novelty and importance

## Genuine robotics use and exact scope

The independently read, pinned [OpenArm KER data-collection tutorial](https://github.com/enactic/openarm/blob/e15e09e056098348d6f437389bbf48179caa82b6/website/docs/tutorial/data-collection-ker.mdx) describes KER as the motorless leader that controls OpenArm robot arms during data collection. Its zero-position troubleshooting explicitly directs the operator to put KER in the box, fix it with screws, and **then** use the display's Zero Reset All/confirmation to write zeros.

Thus the real usefulness bridge is mechanical preparation of a robot teleoperation/data-collection system for a repeatable zero reference. This is stronger than calling an arbitrary object a robot component. Version21's appropriate category is **Impact on AI timelines: automating robot building/data-center work**. No employment totals are borrowed. The physical task's claimed endpoint remains **calibration-ready mechanical posture**, not written encoder offsets, accurate sensor output or a completed data-collection setup. Encoder readings are not required to observe this manufacturer's preparation phase.

The mechanical subset is plausible but not automatically sufficient for importance: it must preserve consequential multi-link datum registration and released retention. If it reduces to an object placed on a tray followed by unrelated screws, a robotics brand/context does not rescue it.

## Bounded conclusion

Searches covered fixture, datum, calibration, kinematic and robot-fastening terms ([records](evidence/official-searches.json));46 fixture hits and multiple pages mean this is not exhaustive board/semantic clearance. **Importance is credibly source-backed; novelty remains CONDITIONAL**, especially against #1900. No official duplicate verdict is asserted.

The defensible candidate ability is **establishing and preserving the prescribed posture of an articulated leader across actual independent datums while changing support/retention**. Before investing in a contact prototype, the registered KER kinematic state must show that this ability is real and consequential. A one-pipe seat test or body-lock test alone cannot demonstrate it. This novelty uncertainty contributes to the current HOLD; it is not a claim that genuine CAD automatically meets review criteria.
