# Three detailed plans for later implementation

These are proposed configurations and implementation designs for **N040, N044 and N047**, not created task code or demonstrated robot trials. All three passed the research screen. They remain subject to the cheap source/access/mechanism gates below. No GPU, native run, paid test, procurement or simulation prototype was started.

Source records and full plans: [N040](evidence/parallel-20/mechanics_b_deep.json), [N044/N047](evidence/parallel-20/mechanics_a_deep.json). Exact vehicle/SST/parts information is read from a pinned CHARM reproduction, not authenticated live Hyundai. All current-part supersession/VIN claims and unmeasured dimensions stay explicit.

## Plan 1 — N040: remove genuine trim for power-window service access

**Target configuration.** A proposed 2013 U.S. Hyundai Elantra UD1.8L left-front door with its matching full-size trim, genuine clip seats/retainers, upper-edge engagement, inside-handle cage and all fitted rear electrical connections. Select and record an actual donor VIN/trim, panel and clip identifiers before geometry work; the archive does not establish identical hardware on every trim. An ordinary **plastic panel-removal tool** is explicitly source-supported. Bind an actual tool/SKU and its blade/handle rather than inventing an oversized lever.

Sources: [p863](https://github.com/Kmberry1989/Elantra/blob/0f7b23efdcf6ecc48919fb781be7e95a1b8f0f06/2013%20Hyundai%20Elantra%20L4-1.8L/pages/863.html), [p887](https://github.com/Kmberry1989/Elantra/blob/0f7b23efdcf6ecc48919fb781be7e95a1b8f0f06/2013%20Hyundai%20Elantra%20L4-1.8L/pages/887.html); images945643715,945655092,945669489,945681280 were read/viewed. The full perimeter, handle cage and rear plugs are real parts of this procedure. O*NET 30.2 Core 23546 plus704,640 mechanics supports the actual window-service context. The task is an important access handoff, not a claim to complete motor replacement.

**Normal bounded start.** The prerequisite quadrant cover and normal mounting screws may already be removed and explicitly listed as out of scope. Real task clips start engaged. The handle cage, plugs and normal cable slack remain connected. The door shell can use an ordinary documented repair support at a valid service pose, with actual access-relevant flange/window/body geometry retained. No fixture may hold the trim, pull its clips, hide a removed edge or replace a door with a small hinged plate. An ordinary padded surface may receive the detached panel.

**Actual action/endpoint.** Acquire a real tool contact behind the clip load path; control release while supporting the panel; disengage the actual handle cage and plugs in the source-compatible manner; place the genuinely detached panel on independent support. Do not silently simplify a cage into an invented Bowden-cable release. The source procedure does not make a harness a permitted support sling.

**Proposed implementation approach, not implemented.** Use actual-scale panel/flange/clip/edge geometry, a reduced compliant panel and calibrated local retaining-clip contact model. A faithful disclosed approximation is allowed: it need not be an exhaustive finite-element door. Tool contacts must cause actual spring/tab disengagement and panel reaction; proximity, time, tool identity or controller phase must not “pop” a latch. Rear plug/cage latches and ordinary harness slack remain physical. A future expert may plan from simulator state and act only through robot joints/tools.

**Physical measurement/scoring design.** Full completion is panel separation from every retained interface in the declared scope, intact independently supported trim, sound door/handle/wire/plug seats and released robot support. Inspectable partial milestones can count actually released clip sites, then clips released with real remaining attachment work. Use before/after scaled photos, specific seat views, ordinary independent movement check and straightedge/profile reference for permanent bending; no score from panel displacement alone or arbitrary robot-home pose.

No clip count, panel mass, bend tolerance, force threshold or plug count has been invented. The source tells the mechanic to replace damaged clips; it does not prove universal reuse. First verify the selected ordinary procedure can preserve the intended retainers. If a part is genuinely consumable, declare/reset it honestly with source support; that does not license tearing the panel, breaking seats or wires as a scoring shortcut. Small cosmetic differences need proportionate, reproducible treatment, not a vague pristine requirement.

**Critical YAM issue.** Full door-perimeter, upper-edge and rear-plug swept access with one arm supporting the real panel and one holding the tool. Nominal94mm jaw aperture is irrelevant to whether the palm fits between panel and door. Preserve the actual full-size reach rather than shrinking the panel. This is medium/high complexity, not a trivial one-clip rigid task.

## Plan 2 — N044: seat a real rear crankshaft oil seal

**Target configuration.** The full relevant rear block/installed crankshaft/flange geometry of the documented 2013 Elantra1.8L engine, ordinarily supported for engine service. Use a genuine matched **HYD214432E000** rear main seal as listed in [parts p4278](https://github.com/Kmberry1989/Elantra/blob/0f7b23efdcf6ecc48919fb781be7e95a1b8f0f06/2013%20Hyundai%20Elantra%20L4-1.8L/pages/4278.html), the actual **09231-H1100 /09214-2E000** SST combination, ordinary source-permitted hammer and fresh engine oil on the new seal lip. Read [p265](https://github.com/Kmberry1989/Elantra/blob/0f7b23efdcf6ecc48919fb781be7e95a1b8f0f06/2013%20Hyundai%20Elantra%20L4-1.8L/pages/265.html) and matching shaft/driver diagram680868746. Do not use a guessed SKF interchange or infer physical dimensions from picture scale. Current VIN compatibility/material/supersession remains a binding gate; archive prices are excluded.

**Normal bounded start.** The crankshaft/lower assembly is in the normal preceding rebuild state, with no seal installed; a fresh seal and actual tools start loose. Prior teardown and subsequent engine reassembly are explicitly outside scope. A normal engine stand may carry block reaction. Keep every shaft/flange/lead-in feature relevant to seating and wrist clearance; do not create an open-ring classroom fixture or pre-start the seal perfectly square in the bore.

**Actual action/endpoint.** Lubricate the source-specified interface, start the case square around the shaft, engage the genuine annular driver load path and tap incrementally to the actual rear block-face datum. The nominal endpoint is **flush, axial offset0mm**; the reproduction supplies no justified numerical ±band. The claim is mechanical seal installation, not certified oil tightness or completed engine repair. Core 23532/23544 supports faulty-power-assembly/engine-part repair, while broad crankshaft/block rebuilding23547 is Supplemental and must not be counted as Core.

**Proposed implementation approach, not implemented.** Mostly rigid actual driver/hammer/case/block/shaft geometry, with bounded sealing-lip response and calibrated case interference/friction as needed. Real contacts must govern starting, cocking, driver engagement, progress and retained seat. A disclosed constraint/contact approximation can be proportionate, as accepted assembly tasks show; do not prescribe the final seal pose, weld at a depth threshold or map tap count directly to success. Impact dynamics and lip contacts, not rigid-body count, dominate cost.

**Physical measurement/scoring design.** Measure recess/projection at multiple circumferential locations against the real block datum with a straightedge/depth method. Confirm correct part/orientation, actual retained seating and observable lip/spring/case condition using available normal assembly views or an ordinary inspection mirror where feasible. Use correct, cocked, reversed and deliberately misplaced-interface specimens as meaningful counterexamples. Do not cut a viewing slot into the actual block or require an unsupported hidden-state certificate. If complete hidden lip integrity is not observable, bound the claim and validate the mechanical endpoint; do not silently claim a pose check certifies leakage. A minor uncertainty does not automatically invalidate genuine mechanical assembly, but a counterexample that fully defeats the claimed completion criterion must be resolved.

The numerical flush band, gauge repeatability, tilt criterion, seal OD/ID/width/interference, actual driver/hammer envelope and required impulse remain TBD. They are engineering inputs to measure/read before coding, not facts supplied by a part number. Hands/tools must release the completed seal normally; destructive modification cannot earn progress.

**Critical YAM issue.** Grasp the real seal and driver around the installed shaft/flange while the other arm produces a controlled ordinary tap. Actual contact normal force, grasp slip, handle length and robot pose matter. A7Nm wrist configuration is not guaranteed transferable torque/impact, and50N gripper configuration is not a certified force or100N two-jaw sum. Likely lower precision burden than two valve-keeper halves, but still medium/high contact cost.

A real leverage advantage is legitimate: approximately `F_handle = F_spring × r_contact/r_handle + losses`, with all these selected-equipment inputs still unmeasured. ## Shared stopping rule

The first useful steps are traceable part/geometry binding and inexpensive full-scale access/scoring checks. A coupon may falsify a contact model; it does not prove a complete assembly or full-panel reach. Measurements and forces not in the source remain unknown. No native test is needed to discover a missing real interface, incompatible tool, unobservable claimed endpoint or impossible stock-grasp path.

Simulator-state planning, ordinary instructions and proportionate material approximation are allowed under Version 21. Do not reject merely because a finished native demo is absent at this stage. Conversely, a manual/part number does not pass physical scoring or robot feasibility. These plans justify continued planning, with the [queue](IMPLEMENTATION_QUEUE.md) retaining all five alternatives.
