# Kia SC280: scope, evidence status and reuse policy

**This is Kia Sorento tailgate/rear-view-camera mounting-base service, not a 2013 Hyundai Elantra front-door procedure.** Shared part number does not transfer the vehicle geometry, clip inventory, access sequence or tool path.

## What was actually examined

The uploaded external handoff was read in full. It reports that manufacturer-authored Kia SC280 (October 2023), archived at [NHTSA](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2023/RCRIT-23V610-6691.pdf), identifies clip **82315 2P000** in its required-parts table, up to fourteen if damaged, and directs replacement of clips that cannot be reused. It reports tailgate-trim removal and replacement of damaged clips as necessary on page2, with parts/reuse language on page4.

These are **attributed external inspection findings**, not independent primary-bulletin quotations by this agent. The bulletin itself was not attached. One retrieval of this newly supplied NHTSA URL returned proxy tunnel403; exact filename/SC280/Sorento/clip GitHub mirror searches returned no relevant bulletin. [Retrieval record](evidence/uploaded-catalog-inspection/SC280-retrieval.json), [bounded mirror searches](evidence/uploaded-catalog-inspection/SC280-mirror-searches.json). No former blocked supplier URLs were retried.

The newly inspected supplier catalog separately confirms its cross-reference to82315-2P000. That is not independent inspection of SC280 and does not authenticate every handoff claim.

## What this changes

The handoff provides specific OEM-service evidence, attributed at the correct strength, for **reuse when possible and replacement when damaged** for this part in a real Sorento trim-service context. A universal requirement that every genuine clip survive removal and be reusable is unjustified. So is an assumption that damage is expected or permitted on every removal.

The archived coupon's `valid_nondestructive_release` remains a strict reversible-mechanics diagnostic. It is **not a source-supported universal service-success rule**. Its plastic-set cases must not be relabeled as authentic fastener damage rates or automatically accepted service outcomes. No code or recorded result was changed to make such cases pass.

A later Elantra task would need its own declared replacement/reset policy: distinguish ordinary replaceable-retainer damage from torn carrier/seat/panel, cut or pulled wiring, and unrecovered fragments. Whether a consumed clip requires replacement before the bounded task endpoint depends on the actual procedure. No such policy is invented here. Fourteen is **not** an established Elantra door clip count.

## What it cannot establish

SC280 does not supply the proposed Elantra carrier section, receiving-sheet edge, exact tool-bearing contact, three-dimensional leg connectivity, force/displacement/recovery curve or YAM grasp/access. The user-reported OEM-service use strengthens the part/service and consumability evidence; it does not unblock the contact-model gate. N040 remains PARKED and the vehicle/task scope has not been changed to Sorento.
