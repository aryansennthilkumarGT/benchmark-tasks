## Why the second choice changes

The source leads strengthen the claim that ECHO loading is a genuine operation. They do not establish that we can implement it cheaply or faithfully. Neither its product page nor the supplied PDF was independently retrieved: both requests returned proxy 403. We therefore cannot certify the exact manual/product match, winding direction, line dimensions or feed procedure. More importantly, even a readable manual would not resolve line buckling, capture, coil/contact stability and full-length feed.

N006 offers a comparatively bounded mechanical chain: fit an actual cylinder/cam into the matching lock case, retain it using the genuine screw and confirm operation of the installed lock with the real key. This retains a useful outcome and avoids starting with the flexible material system that makes N085 expensive. It is an estimate based on mechanism, **not evidence that YAM can manipulate the actual key or that reviewers will accept its occupational importance**.

N077 is not selected second today. The new [PR #811](https://github.com/robocurve/benchmark-tasks/pull/811) comparison exposes a specific trap: its code calculates a displayed gap from an independent slider and represents locking/reference via abstract states. The final review calls out the absent physical readhead adjustment and verification. A genuine N077 must use an actual robot encoder installation, retained geometry and a defensible functional endpoint; none is yet source-bound. Rejecting that surrogate is not a blanket rejection of encoder tasks or shared gap-setting motions under Version 21.

## Proposed first choice: N013

Before implementation, require all of the following:

1. Independently read the exact [LAPP sheet](https://products.lappgroup.com/fileadmin/documents/technische_doku/datenblaetter/skintop/BZ99990616DE_EN.pdf); bind an applicable SKU, cable/jacket range, enclosure hole/wall, supplied assembly state, permitted tools and specified torque. 2. Resolve the substantive #1562 comparison using the actual central operation and retained endpoint. Bulkhead mounting and axial jacket retention must be demonstrated, not added as cosmetic extra steps. Re-read current official origin/status before any build; public search absence is not ownership clearance.
3. Verify that the exact operation is regular core work for a qualifying occupation. The electrician count and a broad wiring duty alone cannot prove it. Do not sum roles without shared exact duties or switch to Other merely to evade a failed jobs case.
4. Make a 1:1 actual gland/panel/tool/stock-finger clearance map. Check reaction grip, cap sweep/regrasp, cable torsion and torque transfer. The pinned adapter's roughly 94 mm maximum pad aperture and 7 Nm wrist configuration are not proof of access or part torque.
5. On an available genuine sample, measure documented tightening versus actual retained cable displacement under a justified benign pull, including a source-compatible incomplete control. Record released endpoints. This is a proposed physical check, not performed in this sprint; no purchase was initiated.

**Stop:** if its useful ability reduces to #1562's compression fitting, retire it before scene work. Also stop if authentic access/torque or a physically reproducible retention score fails. Do not rescue it with enlarged flats, hidden attachment, an invented seal step or an unsupported IP certification claim.

## Proposed second choice: N006

Before implementation, require all of the following:

1. Select one actual Euro-profile cylinder, matched lock case/door, stock key, retaining screw and OEM-supported installed posture. Obtain the original installation instruction; verify key/cam positioning, screw/driver and final projection/operation requirements. ABUS was a publisher lead, not a verified product selection.
2. Establish direct routine door-hardware installation evidence for the claimed occupation and current importance standard. Locksmith familiarity alone does not establish a >500k-worker jobs case; carpenter/maintenance relevance needs evidence of actual shared core work.
3. Compare the complete installation to keyed CPU/socket seating and shield interlock (#891), M.2-card retention (#692) and RoboDojo InsertKey, plus current board results and origin. Key insertion alone is not the proposed endpoint, but adding a screw does not automatically create a new ability either.
4. Overlay real stock fingertips/palm and ordinary driver at the actual key head, escutcheon, cylinder grip and screw. Verify simultaneous key/cam rotation and insertion without an enlarged key or oversized empty aperture.
5. Rehearse the genuine mechanical chain on an available sample: install and retain, remove support, operate the actual latch/bolt in the OEM-required directions and remove the key normally. Confirm that a loose, wrongly seated or non-engaged assembly fails the same simple physical check.

**Stop:** if key handling/screw access requires invented controls, the selected case cannot provide a real retained functional endpoint, the operation is equivalent to an earlier task, or the importance case is a niche chore rather than qualifying regular work. Do not implement a generic rectangular block with a success latch.
