# Ten comparatively faster implementation paths

Relative research estimates only; none has measured runtime, native success or a build go decision. This ordering favors compact rigid interfaces and inspectable outcomes, while naming the material/force problem that could reverse the estimate. These are selected from the twenty, not declared low-cost guarantees.

| Order | Concept | Research status | Why comparatively achievable / what could defeat it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | N006 Install a Euro-profile cylinder at the correct cam angle | PROMISING | Few rigid parts; modest cam alignment and functional lock test. Tiny key/screw access and torque may still block. |
| 2 | N013 Assemble a cable gland around a routed cable | PROMISING | Compact panel fixture and a small number of real components; seal compression must stay causal. |
| 3 | N040 Remove a trim retainer without bending its panel | PROMISING | One real pry tool/clip/panel contact problem; panel compliance and damage model remain critical. |
| 4 | N050 Secure a hydraulic hose with a cushioned P-clamp | WEAK | Compact sprung clamp/ear/bolt assembly; may fail novelty as generic clamp fastening. |
| 5 | N077 Set a robot-joint magnetic encoder readhead gap | PROMISING | Mostly rigid geometry with a real setup gauge; submillimetre setting/fastener access remain. |
| 6 | N085 Load nylon line into a bump-feed trimmer head | PROMISING | One genuine service head and repeatable winding mechanism; long flexible line may dominate cost. |
| 7 | N065 Set a folding walker to matching leg heights | WEAK | Passive detents/sliding tubes; full real walker geometry and regular-duty evidence remain. |
| 8 | N095 Load a commercial tape dispenser correctly | PROMISING | Compact real tool; adhesive web threading may make it much harder than the rigid body count suggests. |
| 9 | N005 Snap glazing bead around an installed pane | PROMISING | Few long profile parts; elastic snap and window-system dimensions may dominate. |
| 10 | N098 Replace a flushometer diaphragm assembly | PROMISING | Identifiable service components; functional flow/diaphragm model makes this slower than a plain part swap. |

Before implementation: read the actual manual, reject existing same-ability tasks, and prove the real stock-gripper interface. An apparently simple part count is not a substitute for these checks.
