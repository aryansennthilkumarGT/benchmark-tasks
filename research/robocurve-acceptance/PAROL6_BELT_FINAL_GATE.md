## 3. Exact input hold and cheap robot assessment

**Only the next acquisition is requested:** one source-compatible **dimensioned J4 installation-stage axial section or assembly record** that fixes the motor pulley's track/hub/flange geometry and axial position against the printed output pulley/open elbow, including whether that pulley is axially loose while the belt is fitted. This directly determines whether the essential route is axial slip-on, staged pulley-and-loop placement, rolling a flexible span into engagement, or an inaccessible path. A generic12T drawing without installed registration would not close the hold; neither would another photograph without a scale/reference plane.

The input is recoverable in principle from a publisher installation drawing/CAD section or an accurately dimensioned genuine installation record. **Its public availability was not established.** We are not proposing a new multi-sprint reverse-engineering project or authorizing purchases to obtain it. If it is unavailable, park this candidate.

The existing YAM source bundle was loaded unchanged: **315 collision hulls per arm** are available. Actual grip/sweep evaluation was refused with **`NOT_RUN_MISSING_REGISTERED_J4_AXIAL_STACK`**. The component audit is not advertised as an access test. No empty-environment IK,20 numerical-control replay, point-TCP path or nominal aperture comparison was substituted for it.

The physical issues that this one section must resolve are concrete:

- The6mm belt must be pinched on a real free span without a finger occupying a flange, hub or elbow wall. A large gripper's jaw aperture does not establish thin-belt purchase or allow its palm/wrist to pass the opening.
- The first captured segment must stay engaged while the remaining loop is routed. Both arms cannot simultaneously hold two belt spans **and** a free unstable subassembly unless the source-compatible bench/support/contact sequence actually permits it. No invisible third hand or oversized fixed mock-up is allowed.
- The authentic lower-arm/elbow structure and already routed wires/tubes must occupy their real places. The manual lists4×2.5mm pneumatic tube and wire preparation/routing, but a hole that can hold a bundle is not proof of collision-free gripper access beside it.
- At a future geometry pass, use the actual-scale registered parts plus full YAM fingertips, palm, wrist and opposing arm through grasp, first capture, second capture, support transfer, roller fastening and release. No fixed-drive support or carried mass is invented. Publisher dynamics include inherited/estimated values; they are not a measured installation-subassembly mass.

Once geometry is bound, a source-grounded reduced flexible loop with finite bending/axial compliance and explicit tooth/flange contacts is a reasonable **prototype study**, with uncertain properties varied and reported. It must allow loss of capture, twist, tooth skipping and excessive strain. No rectangular rigid loop, split belt, scripted wrap or engagement-at-depth shortcut is acceptable. Material/friction calibration and grip-load validation remain later qualification work, not results supplied by the section or by this audit.

## 4. Externally observable completion and failure separation

The following is a **proposed physical scoring design**, not an implemented or validated scorer. Exact acceptance tolerances must follow matched geometry and measurement resolution, not another PR's settings.

| Outcome | Observable distinction / proposed credit |
|---|---|
| Neither pulley genuinely engaged; loop merely nearby/on flange tops |0. Inspect exposed tooth/track edges from the open side; proximity or enclosing the shaft axes is insufficient. |
| One pulley captured, other still unseated |Provisional0.25 only if the captured segment follows that pulley through at least one tooth pitch without riding its flange.12T/48T nominal pitches are30°/7.5°. Record which pulley; do not infer engagement from a hidden flag. |
| Both pulleys seated, but roller installation incomplete |Provisional0.50: backing/edge witness marks show a continuous untwisted loop, belt width lies in both tracks and teeth occupy grooves. A belt on tooth tips or hanging over one flange fails this stage. |
| Both tracks and source-compatible tension rollers installed |Provisional0.75 after roller pair sizes/locations and actual screw retention are declared and inspected. A cosmetic roller or a floating screw does not count. |
| Correct retained completion after both hands release |Full credit would additionally require the declared product-specific tension acceptance and a benign hand-driven tracking check. Mark both pulleys: nominal4 motor revolutions per1 output revolution provides a tooth-registration/skip check. Check no drift, walking, twist or loss of seating after release. This is an installation check, not powered torque/encoder calibration. |

A twisted/displaced/damaged belt or wrong routing overrides apparent seating; inspection uses the visible tooth edges, backing witness marks and tracking, not a top-view silhouette alone. Partial axial seating must be checked at both edges of **both** tracks; one engaged edge and the other on a flange is not full engagement. Missing capture fails transmitted rotation/registration even if the assembly looks close.

**Over-tensioning cannot presently be distinguished by a justified numeric pass band.** Record span deflection under a known force or a belt-frequency/force measurement after release, then compare with a genuinely applicable belt/drive acceptance specification or physical calibration. The manual's bearing choices do not provide that band. Do not call low sag “correct” or use #2203's force/band. Visible damage or binding is a failure, but absence of those signs is not proof of acceptable preload. Consequently a full-score hardware rule is **not finalized**. This is a later task-qualification obligation, explicitly separate from the one immediate axial-geometry input needed to decide whether a CPU installation coupon is worth building.
