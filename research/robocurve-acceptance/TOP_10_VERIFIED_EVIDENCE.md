## Complete ECHO loading: skeptical feasibility assessment

The user's description identifies a useful genuine operation: align the marks, pass line through the head, balance the tails and wind. The two blocked ECHO URLs are exact source leads, **not independent confirmation** that this PDF applies to the specific current Speed-Feed 400 unit. Before selecting hardware, check the manual's title/product coverage, head part number, revision, accepted line cross-section/diameter/cut length, mounting state, winding instruction and required feed check. Do not infer any of those values from the filename or similar heads.

A complete task has five coupled physical stages:

1. Begin with a genuinely empty/unthreaded head and a full manufacturer-specified cut length of real line. Align the actual knob/eyelet guides without starting with a line already inside the hidden passage.
2. Pinch and present the line end through the real inlet and internal path. Tip curvature, line memory, unsupported length, internal passage bends and friction determine whether pushing advances the tip or buckles the free span. A visually aligned external eyelet does not prove a straight internal bore.
3. Draw line through until the two external tails meet the actual balance rule. Both tails need physical workspace and contact; do not delete the trailing length or teleport it through the other eyelet.
4. Hold the actual body/knob reaction and produce the proper relative winding while managing tails, grasp changes, fill and torque. Real capture and coil formation must happen before any wound state is scored.
5. Release and verify the correct ready-to-use leader condition and actual feed/retention function using the selected OEM method. A turn count or hidden “loaded” flag cannot establish that the line is free of crossed wraps and will feed.

**Relative rotation must be defined correctly.** Choose a documented viewpoint with the positive axis pointing outward from the loading face, record body and knob angles in that frame, and use `theta_rel = unwrap(theta_knob - theta_body)`. The permitted sign and angle remain **UNVERIFIED** until the exact instruction is read or genuine hardware is inspected. Turning the body while holding the knob can produce the same relative winding as turning the knob oppositely while holding the body; a scorer should recognize an OEM-permitted equivalent strategy. Rotating both together should not create a coil. “Clockwise” without a viewing direction and selected variant is inadequate.

**Why rigid-body count is a poor cost estimate.** Unknown line diameter `d`, bending stiffness `EI`, initial curvature, torsional response, friction, guide radii, spool pitch, full length and winding torque affect every stage. For a circular section, `I = pi*d^4/64`; an idealized buckling load scales as `EI/L^2`. These are sensitivity relations, not numerical predictions for this unknown polymer and boundary condition. They show why shortening a free tail or making the line artificially rigid can turn a difficult threading task into a false easy one. Bending strain scales approximately as `d/(2R)` in a simple bend, so small guide radii cannot be ignored. Contact count and fill geometry grow with the actual full length, even with only a handful of rigid head components.

A disclosed rod, quasistatic or constraint approximation may be acceptable under Version 21 if it preserves push buckling, passage friction/capture, relative winding and functional feed. It needs calibration and counterexamples. Reject prescribed coil poses, motor-angle-to-wound-length counters that bypass line motion, pre-positioned internal line and enlarged guides that remove threading. Conversely, a specific solver family or a perfectly continuous material model is not an acceptance requirement.

**Inexpensive kill sequence, not performed:** measure real push force versus unsupported length and stock-grip slip; then capture one genuine turn with both tails and wrong-sign/no-relative-turn controls; then test several fill levels including full specified length and actual feed. A short first-turn coupon is a disqualification experiment, not evidence of complete feasibility. Record setup, force/torque traces, line state, tail management and failures. If the full operation requires scripted internal rearrangement, retire this representation before native testing. If it succeeds physically but no validated CPU material model exists, keep it on hold rather than making a GPU-readiness claim.

## Common cheap gates and physically defensible scoring

These are proposed experiments and decision rules, not implemented scenes or performed hardware tests. No purchase or paid run was initiated.

| Order | Gate | Evidence required before proceeding | Early stop |
|---|---|---|---|
|0|Source and importance desk check|Exact compatible product/revision and readable procedure; direct core-duty evidence and justified category; origin-aware comparator check.|Wrong manual/variant, niche duty without a defensible category, or equivalent existing task.|
|1|1:1 access overlay|Real part/tool dimensions, assembled surroundings, transformed stock-gripper envelope, every required grasp/rotation and reaction point.|Only enlarged handles, removed real obstructions or a reduced-scale product permit access.|
|2|Benign real-sample mechanism check, when available|One actual critical engagement; retained endpoint and deliberately incomplete counterexample; permitted tool forces.|Mechanism behaves unlike proposed model or fails with genuine stock access.|
|3|CPU analytical/material check, only after a separate implementation decision|Measured parameter ranges and sensitivity; for flexible tasks, actual passage/coil/closure behavior and negative controls.|Success depends on artificial stiffness, hidden attachment or state flags disconnected from geometry.|
|4|Whole procedure and physical scoring rehearsal, before any later native request|Full size, full length, ordinary work posture, released endpoint; two observers can apply the same simple measurement.|Coupon success does not extend to full workpiece, scoring cannot distinguish loose/crossed/failed assemblies, or endpoint requires unsupported certification.|

Numeric tolerances, proof loads, speeds and torque ranges must come from the selected OEM/source or an explicitly justified benign measurement protocol. None is invented here. Use an unambiguous fail/partial/full rule covering incomplete, dropped, damaged and wrong-interface outcomes; final retention/operation requires hands released when appropriate. Earlier stages may earn partial credit only for genuine retained progress; destroying an assembly or bypassing the interface must not earn progress. These are requirements for future scoring, not a scorer implemented in this sprint.

Source acquisition can eliminate candidates before any hardware spend. Physical rehearsal and later CPU prototyping are proposed next gates; they are not authorization to start implementation. A readable manual alone passes neither importance, novelty, physical scoring nor robot feasibility.
