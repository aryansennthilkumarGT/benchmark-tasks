# Source and assumptions

Base research commit: `3c0a99a80408e6f80c6a6c7cbd01ea827af12e34`. Shell, clean branch and fetched origin checkpoint were actually verified before writing code. All new work is confined to this implementation directory.

## YUBI: exact hardware geometry, unsuccessful access unit

Original Toyota pin `dd8bd13d2fd8e5003057243576f88be333d95fc5`; full assembly SHA256 `7cffdf898a1cf54b897e95c116aa9dea6ac4307c06964d551001ecf938f321dd`. The loader checks that hash and preserves 46 occurrence transforms, excluding only the Quest controller installed after step11. Forty-five original hardware bodies, including FLAP_R, remain in collision checks. CAD millimetres are converted to metres once.

The known finger pivot/hook come from the source analytic faces. The rear fork candidate uses original faces 276–279 and neighbouring groove 253. Its proposed loop-centre point was checked against the actual solid: **it is in/on the original solid**, so it is not a validated free-band centreline. This hypothesis was not promoted into a contact mechanism. The exact saddle route/offset remains unresolved; identifying a CAD feature is not proof of the installed loop path.

The loose trial band is 64 unanchored spring-linked nodes with gravity and unilateral floor contact. Trial radius 19 mm, section radius 0.55 mm, mass 10 mg/node and spring parameters are **exploratory**, not verified No16 dimensions/material data. The BOM identifies No16; its actual product dimensions and constitutive response remain unverified. No band is attached to a hook or gripper in this unit. The ordinary blunt-pick envelope has a 9 mm handle, 2 mm shaft and 1.6 mm blunt tip; contact acquisition/wrench capacity was not established. No custom robot fingers or deleted glove hardware were used.

The bounded experiments use two declared fixture placements and one working arm with the other parked, both source arm geometries included. They do not exhaust bimanual plans or every ordinary tool. Fixed support through the genuine grip is a collision-fixture hypothesis, not a force-validated clamp. No universal YUBI impossibility claim follows.

## Faze4: exact published profiles, failed rigid closure

Publisher pin `a482f3993457fd5b4071d740f0bc826ffc0d7b32`; J4 meshes are read from the existing pinned `STL_V2` evidence. The real disk, case, eccentric, carrier and cover are sectioned. Twelve ring centres are extracted from the 32 mm-radius through-hole pattern; five output centres from the carrier; disk holes use the source STEP radius 5.503029 mm. Roller radius 4 mm follows the specified 3×8×4 bearings. Approximately 1.499962 mm eccentricity follows the independently fitted shaft seats.

The planar model assumes a centred output bearing, rigid disks/rollers and the source-relative eccentric centres. It searches phases rather than prescribing a ratio. It does **not** establish the full axial stack, compliant printed fits or assembled cover-fastener registration. The roughly 0.4 mm residual interference is a failure of this nominal rigid registration—not proof that the designer's physical robot cannot work. We did not enlarge holes, remove required rollers or invent compliance to obtain a pass.

The cover diagnostic aligns central axes only. At nominal z = 25 mm the case/cover interiors are separated by their Z bounds; triangle-mesh contact depth on the coincident interface is **not** a solid penetration measurement. The intentionally lowered cover has 1 mm of Z-envelope overlap. Neither observation establishes complete cover compatibility.

## Microwave coupon: verified topology, explicitly representative dimensions

Original branded Toshiba ML2-EC09SAIT(BS) manual, EN16/PDF19, read through the attributed RealAppliance mirror at `fb7bd3c6ad7e26472d89719efe55d281cff8c528`; PDF SHA256 `75e5a79cce24638a60ba4add9cccfb2ae40aa666d95a79423d498b9e97f86f5d`. This is not a fresh authenticated Toshiba-site retrieval.

The source establishes a triangular three-wheel carrier in the floor recess, correctly engaged glass tray with its depicted three-lug underside, right-way-up orientation and free rotation. It does **not** dimension the lug/flank section, drive, floor recess, glass, mass, cavity or door.

Accordingly, **every numerical geometry/material value in this coupon is authored and unvalidated against the Toshiba donor**. [parameters.json](toshiba/parameters.json) exposes the principal values:270mm tray,5mm thickness,0.70kg glass,100mm wheel orbit,12mm wheel diameter and an interdigitating three-feature coupling. Narrow convex sectors represent the male arms and glass lugs; the floor recess is a shallow circular seat. These preserve a realizable support/transmission topology; they are not reconstructed OEM CAD or proof of compatibility.

The floor and cavity are fixed. The carrier is a free body; its three wheels have genuine hinges. The tray has a free joint. Input torque reaches it only through contact; only the input-shaft velocity actuator receives commands. MuJoCo's rigid contact/friction regularization and stiffness are numerical assumptions, not measured glass/plastic behaviour. No fracture, wear or motor electrical model is implemented. Geometry sensitivity includes a7mm drive-arm top that fails coupling, showing why the unknown real section matters.

The separate YAM screen uses a representative 400×360×240 mm cavity, left-hinged door at 90/110 degrees, ordinary support rods, station table and authored appliance placement. It preserves the door obstruction. Every arm's 315 original hulls are retained, including fingertips, palm, wrist and upstream links. Expected fingertip/tray contacts are declared; palm/door and arm/fixture contacts are not exempted. Adjacent-link exemptions follow the existing validated preflight. Sampling is not continuous swept certification, and a prescribed carried-object pose is not a demonstrated physical grasp.
