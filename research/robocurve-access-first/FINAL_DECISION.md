# Final decision

**B — NO SURVIVING CANDIDATE. Recommendation: STOP implementation.**

The bounded evaluation is complete. This is not a proof that the real operations are globally impossible; it is a rejection of the tested plans and current source packages from implementation selection.

- **ODRI:** the original support/base intersection is confirmed independently in STEP. Four rolled/shifted support templates remove that collision at finite samples, but the tested acquisition solve is unresolved and the required working-gripper/support-forearm configurations collide. Physical foot/tool purchase, complete tread geometry, one-annulus installation and released retention are unproved. No complete swept certificate exists.
- **Hamilton Beach:** the manual supplies a genuine loaded-spit procedure, but the matched donor spit/end profiles, right socket, left cradle, cavity/door/heater layout and roast envelope are missing. The researcher asset metadata/control code does not establish them. Creating a working scene would require invented essential geometry.
- **Ninja:** the manual supplies the seating/folding-handle/under-tab procedure. The available package lacks calibrated original dimensions and an identified authentic handle-to-catch mechanism. Generic lid placement cannot stand in for that operation. Exact-model external-benchmark overlap is also unresolved.
- **Opentrons:** newly read manufacturer data establishes a full 84 cm-tall Flex and its 14 cm roof module. The 216 point/orientation queries yield168 necessary-sphere exclusions,43 unresolved IK solves and5 free-space IK solutions. None identifies a real socket/clip pose or supplies the actual enclosure/gantry/lamp geometry, acquisition or continuous path. A detached low face-up coupon would change the documented installed-on-Flex task.
