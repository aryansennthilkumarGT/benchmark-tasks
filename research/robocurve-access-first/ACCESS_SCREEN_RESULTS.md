## Opentrons numerical survey

The newly read manufacturer Flex specifications give 87×69×84 cm and 88.5 kg. Its module is 87×64×14 cm, approximately 20 kg. The installation requires attaching the module to Flex before inserting the bulbs underneath, with gantry parked as needed. A low detached face-up lamp bay would change this task.

Two authored stationary-bench heights, 0.7475/0.90 m, were used. On the lower bench the robot roof is 1.5875 m. Targets cover an **optimistic 14 cm region below the roof**, rather than fabricated socket anchors. Three positions along each horizontal axis, two heights, two arms and three orientations yield 216 queries.

- Outside the source-URDF necessary TCP sphere: **168 queries**. This exclusion is rigorous for those point queries, not for all possible workcell layouts.

- IK solved: **5**. The known table/arm geometry is clear for these points; missing Flex enclosure/gantry, exact sockets, lamp and clip prevent any full access result.

Queries sharing a target with different orientations are not independent physical trials. No socket positions, body grasp, lamp transport or continuous certificate was inferred from point reach.
