## Main unresolved disagreements

1. **Ninja091 replacement is defensible, not unconditionally cleared.** Reinspection of all exact queries/GT plus model091/utils.py finds no handle/latch-retention demand. RealAppliance's defined task is action planning/next action for food/control operations; Ninja tests causal lever-driven retention under physical load after release. The same-object `close(lid)` overlap is real and a strict reviewer may construe proper closure broadly; disclose that objection. Do not describe generic closure as absent, or claim source geometry is complete. OpenMV was excluded because active#346 already explicitly demands physical image-feedback focus. Its Nov16 appeal reverted final rejection to revision and current head adopts the corrections. [Current chronology](critic/pr346.txt).
2. **Create3 caster** has an explicit negative recommendation from its source investigator; tiny retainer handling remains unproved.
3. **HEPA underside access** must remain the actual mounted service operation; an upside-down easy fixture is a different skill.
4. **ODRI optical foot** needs more than twelve insertions to establish distinction; authentic sensing mechanics and centering must remain.
5. **YUBI return scoring** is a proposed functional assay. Do not attribute numerical angle/time tolerances to the manufacturer.

Rejected/demoted alternatives were not used to fill this list: Create3 faceplate versus#34/#1964 keyed twist retention; Heater-Shaker versus#1283 screw-driven transverse clamp; OpenMV focus versus active#346; generic breadpan/burr bayonets; Black+Decker twin-beater identity/two-peg rationale.

### Final replacement decision and CPU-report boundary

The final table replaces OpenMV with Ninja091; the fifteen are otherwise unchanged. The YES judgment is a defensible semantic distinction, not a claim that the external suite lacks generic lid closure or that all broader competitors are cleared. Exact source text does not establish an over-center cam; the physical model must retain genuine handle-driven under-tab capture with disclosed uncertain linkage dimensions.

Root separately reports execution of a bounded YUBI source-geometry access test:28 candidate poses,630 actual YAM collision hulls against45 STEP-body envelopes; all28 returnedINCONCLUSIVE, with a minimum of3 envelope overlaps. Far-clear and palm-overlap controls behaved as expected. These are **root-reported execution results**, not a critic-run path test. Narrow-phase follow-up is pending at this review update. There is no band installation, contact-force or YAM-feasibility pass. Do not convert an envelope inconclusive result into either a demonstrated collision-free grasp or a proven impossibility.


## Root resolution after the independent review

The final scores/classifications in TOP_15_VERIFIED_TASKS.md govern investment. Original investigator dissent is retained. OpenMV was replaced by the specifically handle-actuated Ninja task, with actual RealAppliance091 endpoint overlap explicitly preserved. The critic judged the required physical capture/pull-test ability different from the inspected action-planning definitions; this remains a strict-review risk, not an exhaustive novelty clearance.

The root subsequently ran the bounded YUBI screen.28/28 conservative envelope cases were inconclusive. OCCT narrow phase of the least-overlapping case proved26.395mm³ of actual right-finger/FLAP_R penetration. That direct-hand pose is rejected; other approaches/tools, band acquisition, continuous clearance and spring function are untested. The final YUBI score is84, down from88. This is independent new negative evidence, not an adversarial-review pass.

The15 comprise7 finite CPU-preflight investments,4 source-gated alternatives and4 high-risk reserves. Reserve inclusion is not permission to overlook a hard gate. Create3 retains the investigator's not-top15 dissent; root retains it only as a documented, distinct high-risk alternative. The source-rich old-work-box, filter, Heater-Shaker and OpenMV alternatives did not receive artificial upgrades to pad the implementation queue.
