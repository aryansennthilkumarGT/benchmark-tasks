# Three practical build blueprints

These are plans for finite CPU studies, not implemented tasks or permission for paid testing. Shared infrastructure is the existing [YAM preflight](../robocurve-acceptance/preflight/README.md); do not rebuild its kinematics or substitute fingertip reach for clearance. Proposed file names below describe future work. None was added to the official task tree.

## 1. Toyota YUBI glove: install the real return-spring loop

**Configuration.** Use the right-hand ver2 glove from `Toyota/yubi-hw@dd8bd13d2fd8e5003057243576f88be333d95fc5`, matching its assembly STEP and guide. Start immediately before step11: geared fingers, plates, flaps, holder and prior electronics assembled; the Quest controller is absent because its installation is step12. Supply the BOM's single No16 natural-rubber band, detached. No encoder mounting, zeroing or controller calibration is performed. A normal bench cradle supports genuine grip/plate surfaces without holding either finger or the band. A vertical finger pivot is a declared assembly-fixture orientation that prevents gravity alone from imitating a spring return.

**Critical geometry and contacts.** Preserve both real hooks, the holder groove and exit surfaces, paired band runs shown in the source photograph, finger/gear coupling, adjacent FLAP_R, plate and camera/holder obstructions. Registered CAD is available; it is not necessary to invent a hand-sized replacement hook. The finger pivot is source-bound at `(-11.690497,-10.080052,1020)`mm, along+Y. Analytic finger-hook face5 has centre `(-22.055430,9.919948,1035)`mm and area2.5403mm². The holder-hook saddle has **not** yet been unambiguously identified; the photo establishes its role, not a numerical routing point. Identify it from the existing CAD before calculating extension or torque.

**Mechanism.** Begin with one closed elastic strip/rod loop with axial elasticity, bending resistance, contact and friction. The loop must acquire hooks and groove by contact, stretch under manipulation, slip out if misplaced and exert torque on the real moving finger. A reduced compliant rod is permissible if its actual section, route and failure modes are retained and its stiffness/friction assumptions are swept. No fixed attachment, installed flag, commanded return angle or collision-disabled groove. No coil, timing belt or open tendon substitute.

**Approximations.** Printed texture, cosmetic electronics detail and the constitutive law may be simplified and declared. Band width, thickness, free circumference, hysteresis and friction remain unverified; the BOM product identity does not prove those numbers. A first study may parameterize them, but cannot assert compatibility with a real GE011 from an assumed length. The hook undercuts, adjacent flap, route and moving joint are essential.

**Stock-YAM purchase and tools.** An ordinary tray can expose the loose loop; it must not pre-stretch it onto a hidden guide. Check an actual pinch of the band between the unmodified pads, including acquisition and release. One hand steadies or opens the actual finger while the other routes the loop. If direct pads cannot enter, an ordinary blunt hook/pick held by its normal handle is a legitimate alternative to screen; include tool acquisition, anti-rotation purchase, tip/band contact and wrist sweep. The source uses hands; this proposed tool is not an OEM instruction. Do not glue the band to the tool or add large pull tabs.

**Executed geometry finding.** The bounded study in [cpu_preflight](cpu_preflight/README.md) tested28 static source placements against630 actual robot hulls and45 source occurrence envelopes. All28 were inconclusive under conservative envelopes. Exact CAD narrow phase of the least-overlapping pose found **26.395mm³ of FLAP_R/right-finger penetration**. That selected direct-hand pose is rejected. Other grasps/tool paths were not tested. This result lowered the comparative score from88 to84; it does not justify removing FLAP_R or claiming that every possible route fails.

**Minimum next CPU coupon.** First resolve the holder saddle from the same pin and export its contact neighbourhood. Use one finger/holder assembly, real loop and an actual gripper or ordinary pick. Demonstrate physical first-hook capture, stretch/route, second-hook capture and tool withdrawal. Before extensive material tuning, run a full-hull purchase/withdrawal test around the known flap. Then drive the actual finger through a source-compatible range and measure the loop's torque direction and retention. The guide says fingers should open/close smoothly; it specifies no numerical return angle/time.

**Negative controls.** Missing band must produce no band torque; evaluate with gravity neutralized. One hook only, band across the wrong groove side, a slack oversized loop, and a loop pinched by a gripper must fail released completion. A stretched loop held above the hook must not be captured by proximity. A wrong route that jams the finger must fail even if both endpoints look correct. Model/inspect actual breakage or permanent deformation rather than an arbitrary force-damage flag.

**Scorer.** Hierarchical released states: first genuine hook capture; both captures and correct route; then hands-clear cycling without escape or binding. Full score requires physical hook/groove engagement and the declared nondamaging motion/retention test at the same setting. External photographs and a controlled manual/spring-scale finger sweep support the physical measurement. Calibrate any proposed return/torque limits; do not attribute them to Toyota. Inspect route before and after cycling so an incorrectly retained but functional-looking loop cannot pass.

**Proposed modules.** `source_inputs.py` verifies hashes/revision and units; `glove_frames.py` registers bodies/axes/hooks; `stock_access.py` uses existing robot hulls; `loop_coupon.py` implements one causal compliant loop; `measurements.py` defines inspectable route/cycle measurements; `negative_controls.py` records actual results. Only after these pass should a separate native scene and expert be considered.

**Finite investment/kill conditions.** Spend the first CPU work block on acquisition, hook interpretation and withdrawal, rather than polishing visuals. Stop this task if no unmodified hand or ordinary held tool can acquire/release the real-scale band without the source flap/holder blocking it; if the apparent hook interpretation is wrong; if correct routing works only with an arbitrary large loop/material value; or if contact retention requires a hidden constraint. The next independent backup is Faze4, not another glove variation. No GPU investment follows automatically.

## 2. Faze4 J4: close a paired cycloidal reducer

**Configuration.** Match only the compact J4 parts from `Source-Robotics/Faze4-Robotic-arm@a482f3993457fd5b4071d740f0bc826ffc0d7b32`. The manual says J4 uses the same assembly as J5; do not mix the large forearm housing with J4 disks/carrier. Housing, motor, ring rollers, eccentric and lower bearing-equipped disk are initially assembled. The upper bearing-equipped disk, genuine five-pair output carrier and real cover/bearing are loose. This is a documented later assembly stage, not an invented reducer.

**Geometry.** Source bounds: housing77×76.737×25mm; disk58.768×58.296×7mm; carrier46×45.843×20.5mm; cover90×80×14mm. Five output bores have STEP radius5.503029mm on19mm pitch radius; output roller OD is8mm. Independently extracted eccentric seat centres are approximately3mm apart, giving opposing approximately1.5mm offsets. The1.503mm STEP construction offset alone is not proof of eccentricity. Source files are print-oriented, and complete mating transforms have not yet been reconstructed.

**Critical mechanism.** Preserve both orbiting disks, genuine ring contacts, common five paired roller stacks, eccentric/bearing seats and output-cover support. Rotate/settle the second disk at the correct relative phase, acquire the carrier through both disks, close and retain the actual cover, and obtain freely driven reduction. Ordinary rigid contacts and roller-bearing revolute joints are reasonable; a gear constraint that drives output independently of these contacts is not. Do not enlarge orbit-clearance holes to make insertion easy or replace the reducer by static pegs.

**First geometry proof.** Register analytic disk features to the STL frames, then register the two eccentric bearing seats, ring and carrier against source illustrations/stack dimensions. Sweep a complete input revolution with the common output using source contacts. Compare correct phase with a small deliberate phase error. Separate nominal CAD interference from printed tolerances. If source registration cannot form a consistent closed assembly, stop before authoring an expert.

**YAM route.** Screen pinching a7mm disk side strip without crushing a lobe or colliding with the ring, support/regrasp of the46mm carrier, and cover placement while both disks remain captured. Include the palm, wrist, second hand and tool. Five M3 cover screws are a real completion cost: do not claim loose screws are captive in unthreaded cover holes. An ordinary magnetic bit may carry real steel screws if its physical holding interaction is modeled; otherwise acquire/start them normally. The exact head/tool interface must match the chosen source fasteners. A preassembled output roller stack is permitted because the task starts at the manual's reducer-closure stage.

**Scoring.** Before closure, photograph/count **two disks and all five roller pairs**; verify actual part inventory again through a documented post-test inspection if the cap hides it. A single disk can still produce11:1, so ratio alone cannot establish full assembly. Measure cover gap and genuine fastener retention, then input/output witness marks over a full forward and reverse input turn with hands clear. Expected nominal output is approximately32.727degrees per360degree input. Proposed angle/gap limits are benchmark choices, not designer tolerances. Coupling must be contact-driven. Free turn with wrong ratio, correct ratio with a missing disk, cap held by a hand and jammed over-tight cover all fail full score.

**Negative controls.** Wrong upper-disk phase; carrier misses one disk; missing disk; missing roller pair; cap unretained; bearing seized; robot manually turns output; and forced closure with damaged lobes. Correct model must distinguish these without a scripted assembly latch. Reset by opening the real cover and withdrawing carrier/upper disk; inspect all bearing/lobe surfaces.

**Proposed modules.** `source_registration.py`, `reducer_geometry.py`, `orbital_clearance.py`, `yam_acquisition.py`, `rolling_contact_coupon.py`, `physical_measurements.py`, and a negative-control runner. Preserve original CAD license/provenance. The already executed82-STL/4-STEP inspection supplies measurements, not a contact or access pass.

**Kill conditions.** Inconsistent genuine stack; no legitimate stock-hand disk/carrier purchase; all cap-tool paths blocked; nominal correct geometry jams through a full cycle; faithful contacts cannot remain numerically stable without bypassing the rolling mechanism; or the demonstrated skill reduces to ordinary gear phasing already tested by#893/#719. Native work is a later gate only after these finite CPU questions pass.

## 3. Toshiba: seat the driven turntable on its three-wheel carrier

**Configuration.** Use the original ML2-EC09SAIT(BS) procedure EN16/PDF19. Keep its **triangular three-arm carrier**, glass tray, underside three-lug form, actual drive coupler, floor recess, door and cavity walls. The oven is cold/unplugged and accessories begin detached on an ordinary draining surface. No food/heating/microwave radiation simulation is needed for this genuine accessory-installation subtask.

**Geometry and approximation.** The manufacturer illustration establishes topology/sequence, not factory dimensions. Tray diameter/thickness/mass, wheel geometry, coupler flank/depth and cavity dimensions remain unmeasured. A reduced rigid coupon may expose these as explicit representative parameters; it is not a verified Toshiba replica. Use a defensible full-size range and preserve scale-dependent purchase. Before a native task claims this exact donor, source-bind its essential interface/cavity dimensions. Cosmetic knobs and microwave internals can be omitted; the door edge, tray rim, drive and three rolling contacts cannot.

**Mechanism.** Fixed floor/recess, carrier with real freely rotating wheels, and free glass tray with underside geometry. Correct rotational phase permits actual contact seating; wrong phase leaves a detectable proud/wobbling state. No orientation/depth trigger snaps the tray onto the shaft. Friction must allow wheel rolling and possible slip. Gravity supplies retention; no invented lock.

**YAM access.** A full tray will exceed gripper aperture, so compare a real rim pinch with two-arm edge support. An ordinary ridged draining rack may expose an edge; do not supply custom grasp handles. Check acquisition, lift, broad-plate transit through the door opening, underside registration, three-point landing and both hand withdrawals. Both fingertips being near the rim is not proof of a stable purchase or enough glass strength. A stock pad behind the tray must not pass through the oven wall.

**First CPU proof.** One tray/carrier/hub and relevant cavity aperture. Obtain real released seating under gravity; compare wrong phase, missing carrier, one displaced wheel and inverted tray. Use source-guided parameter sweeps to reveal whether a claimed registration challenge disappears or whether pads always collide. Only then add full-arm reach and continuous swept checks in the existing preflight. No native simulation should precede a stable physical scoring corridor.

**Scoring.** Carrier correctly in recess earns partial credit; a tray merely centered earns no full credit. Full outcome includes right-way-up tray, all three wheel supports, engaged underside drive, no rocking/wall contact and free complete rotation after release. Use rim-height measurements and hub/carrier witness marks with macro/mirror inspection or a documented post-trial vertical-lift audit. Establish that this observation method is actually possible before relying on it. A separately instrumented drive-only bench test can prove torque transfer without energizing heaters. Hidden hub-state flags alone are not physical measurement.

**Negative controls and reset.** Wrong lug phase must remain proud or fail drive inspection; missing carrier cannot pass installed height/support; upside-down tray must fail mating; hand-supported tray fails release; free-spinning but disengaged tray fails transmission. Remove tray/carrier and inspect glass/wheels to reset. Cracks/chips or an external prop invalidate success.

**Proposed modules.** `documented_topology.py`, `explicit_dimension_parameters.py`, `tray_hub_coupon.py`, `three_wheel_support.py`, `yam_rim_access.py`, `inspection_protocol.py`, and negative controls. Keep unknowns in a machine-readable input file, not buried constants presented as OEM values.

**Kill conditions.** No full-size stock-pad purchase; no door/cavity corridor; correct and wrong hub seating indistinguishable by the physical scorer; necessary geometry cannot be source-bound before donor-specific implementation; or the operation proves equivalent to#1990's keyed kitchen nesting rather than actual driven rolling support. This is the lower contact-complexity backup, not permission to erase the coupling to gain novelty.
