Bagel button
The bagel feature adds extra time to the toasting cycle to allow for thick-
er breads. You may also use the bagel button for English muffins and
other thick breads.
 1.	Insert slice(s) of bread or halved bagels.
     Be sure that multiple slices do not overlap and the carriage is in the
     up position.
 2. Press the carriage control lever until it locks into position.
 3.	Press the Bagel button to start. Red LED indicates feature is
     activated.
To stop cycle
		  When the bagel cycle is complete, the toaster will raise the bread. If
     you wish to stop the cycle before it is finished, simply press the
