WARNING: When using electrical appliances, basic safety precautions should
always be followed, including:

 1. Read all instructions.

5. Unplug from outlet when not in use and before cleaning. Allow to cool

Before First Use
1.   Remove all packing materials and any removable stickers from the inside
     and outside of the toaster except for the rating label, which should remain
     on the unit.
2.   Gently wipe down the exterior with a damp cloth or paper towel to remove
     any packaging residue. Dry completely.
3.   CAUTION: Never immerse the toaster, cord, or plug in water or any other
     liquid; do not use scouring pads or abrasive cleaners, as they can
     damage the finish.
4.   Unwrap the power cord from the compartment under the base. Place the
     unit on a flat, heat-resistant surface positioned at least 2 inches away
     from the wall or any other objects.
5.   Ensure that the crumb trays are inserted in position at the bottom front
     of the toaster.

Settings and Special Features
The toaster has 4 buttons available and a special anti-jam safety feature that
automatically kicks in if food gets stuck in the toaster.
•   ‌BAGEL: Use for bagels, English muffins, or sandwich rolls. For best results,
     insert food into the toaster with the cut side facing out. This way, the
     toaster applies more heat to the cut side, creating a golden-brown,
     crunchy cut side and soft, chewy outside.
•   FROZEN: Use for frozen foods like frozen bread, waffles, pancakes, or
    toaster pastries. This setting defrosts and toasts simultaneously by
    increasing the toasting time for each shade.
•   ‌+10 SECONDS: Press to add 10 seconds to toasting time. This function
     ensures that you get the exact toast shade you like, even if it falls between
     two of the preset shade settings.
•   ‌EJECT: Use to stop toasting at any time. When the button is pressed, food
     will automatically pop up. When toasting is resumed, the timer will restart
     from its initial time.
•   ‌Anti-Jam Safety Feature: The toaster is equipped with an anti-jam
     feature. If food gets stuck in the toaster, the unit will automatically
     power off. Unplug the unit, remove the food, and plug the unit back in
     to continue toasting.

Toasting Tips
•   ‌Do NOT put buttered bread or pastries with frosting into the toaster, as this
     can create a fire hazard.
•   Never force food into the slots. Food should lower smoothly when the lever
    is pressed down or it could get stuck.
•   ‌Do NOT overcrowd the toasting slots or overlap foods within the slots.
     Each slot is designed to hold 1 piece of standard bread or 1 toaster
     product. Overcrowding or overlapping can cause food to burn or
     toast unevenly.
•   Load bagels with cut sides facing out when using BAGEL mode. This way,
    the cut sides get more heat, ensuring bagels are toasted to crisp-chewy
    perfection.
•   If toasting a single slice, choose one shade lighter than desired, to help
    prevent overbrowning.
•   If using fresh or very thick bread, try choosing a darker shade. Moisture in
    bread slows down toasting, so a longer toast time is required to achieve
    the same shade. For drier or thinner bread, choose a lighter shade.
•   Make quick and easy croutons by toasting your desired bread on a higher
    setting, then crumbling it over salad or soup.
•   Choose thicker slices of gluten-free bread to toast. Store-bought gluten-
    free bread tends to be thin and crumble easily, making it difficult to
    evenly toast and keep in one piece. When shopping for gluten-free bread
    to toast, choose thicker slices with sturdier crusts.
•   When toasting raisin bread or other fruit breads, remove loose fruit from
    the surface of the bread before toasting. This will prevent fruit pieces from
    sticking to the insides of the toasting slots.

Keep It Clean
Your toaster requires very little maintenance besides the regular removal
of crumbs, making it super easy to clean.
1.   Unplug the toaster and cool completely.
2.   Gently wipe down the exterior with a damp cloth or paper towel, if
     necessary. CAUTION: Never immerse the toaster, cord, or plug in water or
     any other liquid; do not use scouring pads or abrasive cleaners, as they
     can damage the finish.
3.   Remove and clean the crumb tray periodically to avoid the buildup of
     crumbs. Pull the crumb tray out from its position at the bottom back
     of the toaster. Wash with a sponge and warm, soapy water and dry
     completely. The crumb tray is NOT dishwasher safe. Be sure to push the
     crumb tray all the way back inside the bottom of the toaster before the
     next use.
4.   Wrap the cord through its compartment underneath the toaster
     before storing.
