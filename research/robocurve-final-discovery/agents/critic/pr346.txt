Load a slide on a microscope, centre the specimen and focus without touching the objective
Category:
Impact on jobs
Run limit:
2,400 steps (2 minutes at 20 Hz)
Task:
Inspect the specimen on the glass slide with the microscope. 1. Load the slide: pull back the black post on the slide holder's lever and hold it, which swings the holder's jaw aside; lay the slide flat in the holder, label on the left, against the two back posts and the left stop; then let the lever go so the jaw closes over the slide's right end. 2. Bring the purple specimen square under the objective, onto the spot of light from the illuminator, with the two drive knobs at the stage's front left. Turning the inner knob clockwise, seen from above, moves the stage away from you; turning the outer knob clockwise moves it to the left. 3. Focus: turn a focus knob on the side of the microscope so that its top moves towards you, raising the stage until the slide's top is 5.2 mm below the objective's front lens (2 mm of travel per 30 degrees). Never let the slide come within 0.5 mm of the objective.
Description:
Loading, centring and focusing a slide is the start of every microscope inspection in a lab. Each step has its own failure: the slide holder snaps shut if let go, the specimen is lost if the stage is driven the wrong way, and racing the stage up cracks the slide and scratches the objective.
Scores:
Dream-1 run: 0.0 (it reached around the microscope but never picked up the slide), demo: 1.0 (one hand held the holder's lever back while the other laid the slide in, then the stage knobs brought the specimen onto the light spot, within 1.5 mm of the objective's axis and the focus knob raised the slide to within 0.5 mm of focus without touching the objective)

Revision, following the review:
Focus target in the field of view.
The slide now carries a 10 mm silicon chip with an alignment mark at its centre: a 1.6 mm cross in 40 um lines with four corner squares. Centring means the mark's centre within 1.1 mm of the objective's axis, inside the 10x field of view (2.2 mm).
Focus can be seen.
A camera monitor, a tablet lying flat on the table between the robots, shows the live 10x view over its whole screen. The mark moves with the stage and is drawn sharp within 10 um of focus, soft within 0.1 mm and blurred within 1 mm.
Focus scored at the depth of field.
Full credit needs the chip within 10 um of focus, the depth of field of the 10x / 0.25 objective; 0.75 within 0.1 mm. A fine focus knob outboard of each coarse knob makes this reachable: coarse to near focus, then fine.
Calibrated real-trial check.
The mark must be in the camera image at 10x. The focus error is the fine-knob travel needed to reach the mark's best focus on the monitor, read off the knob's graduations.
Orientation bug fixed.
The label-left check no longer takes an absolute value, so a slide turned end for end scores 0.
Instruction
now states only the goal.
Category
changed to Other.
Setup, scoring, fidelity and README updated. Dream-1 and demo rerun; new videos and screenshot (media M-M3DPNH).
