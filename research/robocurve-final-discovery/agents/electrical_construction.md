### EC21 — Focus and lock an OpenMV robot-vision camera lens

**HIGH-RISK RESERVE —68/100 (18/14/17/8/11).** Manufacturer-source completeness improved; no CPU or physical feasibility result exists.

**Exact procedure:** [https://github.com/openmv/openmv-doc/blob/f53f84c938fde32bc80d45919c536d651282f209/docs/v4.5.6/_sources/openmvcam/tutorial/hardware_setup.rst.txt](https://github.com/openmv/openmv-doc/blob/f53f84c938fde32bc80d45919c536d651282f209/docs/v4.5.6/_sources/openmvcam/tutorial/hardware_setup.rst.txt). Loosen set screw, screw barrel until the image is sharp, tighten screw. The manufacturer explicitly says the screw creates a bump in the plastic mount; it does not bite directly into lens threads.

**Importance:** OpenMV firmware v4.5.6 sends optical-flow detections to ArduPilot/PixHawk for position control. Its3D AprilTag example explicitly identifies M7/OV7725 with2.8mm lens and3.984x2.952mm sensor. These are genuine robot-vision sources; the conflicting OV5650 constants in another MAVLink example are excluded.

**Inventory/start:** Original M7/OV7725 PCB, standard2.8mm barrel, polymer holder/two fixing screws/set screw; clean sensor and engaged but defocused barrel with lock loose. Mount original board by normal holes on passive fixture. A fixed target at the intended robot viewing distance (e.g.2m as declared benchmark value) and raw camera display provide feedback; ordinary matched Phillips driver rests nearby. Lens projection/thread/holder contact dimensions and mass are not verified. Unmapped manufacturer board drawings are not silently assigned to M7.

**Instruction:** “Focus the robot camera on the fixed target at its marked operating distance, lock the lens with its set screw, and let go.”

**Core ability:** Rotate a tiny real lens barrel while seeing its image, preserve focus against set-screw-induced plastic clamp deformation, and release with retained focus. One hand can counterhold while the other drives; mandatory simultaneous two-hand use remains untested.

**Scoring proposal:** Normalize raw slanted-edge MTF50 to a pre-established physical best-focus image at identical distance/exposure:0.25 at>=0.5,0.5 at>=0.8 without retention. Full1.0 needs>=0.8 after release plus<=2degree lens shift/<5percent MTF loss under declared0.2N tangential pull at measured barrel radius. These are proposed benchmark thresholds, never OEM specs. Damage, fixture/target movement>5mm/2degrees, foreign adhesive/props or contamination zero the run. Reset by loosening and a small safe barrel turn after travel is verified.

**Wrong solutions caught:** Loose but sharp lens fails disturbance; locked but blurred lens fails raw image; moving camera/chart fails fiducials; holding lens for endpoint fails release. Simulation cannot replace optics with an arbitrary focus flag.
