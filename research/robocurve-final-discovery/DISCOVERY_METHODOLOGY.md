## Selection process

Five independent discovery investigations covered robot construction, data-center/lab hardware, electrical/construction, food/warehousing, and care/cleaning. A sixth investigator acted as a critic. The user explicitly authorized parallel investigation in the attached assignment. We expanded beyond the first hundred leads when several initially attractive commercial procedures failed source or ownership checks. Candidate counts and dispositions are reproducible from the structured agent ledgers; the consolidated initial bank distinguishes an inspected procedure from an unverified search lead.

The unit of discovery is an actual operation on identifiable equipment, not a generic verb with a job story attached. Repeated fasteners, extra parts, a different brand and an added final test do not establish novelty. We compare the central manipulation, relevant object roles, setting and completion condition, including legitimate scopes of rejected implementations.

The numerical comparison uses importance /20, novelty /25, source and mechanism /20, YAM and fidelity /20, and scoring and economy /15. These are judgment scores, **not probabilities of acceptance**. Known active duplication and an invented essential mechanism are disqualifying, regardless of score.

## Evidence vocabulary

- **Read:** downloaded bytes or a specific Git object were inspected; exact pin/path/hash is retained where available.
- **Attributed mirror:** an apparently original, branded manufacturer document was read from a third-party repository. We identify that provenance and do not claim a fresh OEM-site verification.
- **Located / blocked:** a URL or citation was found, but its contents were not independently read. It supplies no invented quotation or measurement.
- **Calculated:** derived from specified source geometry or data. A bounding box is not a grasp, a necessary reach bound is not inverse kinematics, and nominal dimensions are not a force calibration.
- **Planning only:** plausible engineering route, with no executed clearance/contact claim.

Many OEM domains, live BLS/O*NET and the taskboard returned proxy403. GitHub smart Git and raw files were usable; public HTML search was intermittently rate-limited. The failed full open-PR crawl is preserved as a failure, not evidence that there are no pending tasks. Finalist checks use current targeted PR pages, pinned heads and actual task/scoring files. ## Importance

Jobs claims require a direct regular Core duty and an appropriate occupation total above500,000. The investigations used the pinned O*NET30.2 task-statement parquet and attributed 2025 national OEWS mirror, preserving source rows and dates. Older CSV snapshots sometimes disagree about Core/Supplemental classification; the newer inspected rows take precedence. Live agency pages were not accessible. Household-only manuals are never justified with restaurant employment totals.

Robot-building claims identify an actual robot, actuator, teleoperation device or robot-lab accessory and the consequence of the assembly. A motorized microscope alone was not accepted as proof of AI-timeline importance. Familiar consumer equipment is evaluated separately under Societal relatability, with niche familiarity disclosed as a rejection risk.

## What readiness means

**BUILD-READY FOR CPU PREFLIGHT** authorizes a finite, small source-guided geometry/contact study. It does not mean YAM access, physical calibration, a native expert, model evaluation or review acceptance has passed. A documented load path and contact topology can justify such a study with explicitly parameterized noncritical dimensions. Perfect OEM CAD is not a prerequisite.

**PROMISING BUT SOURCE-GATED** identifies a particular missing input that could change the operation itself. **HIGH-RISK RESERVE** retains a real, potentially distinct operation whose manipulation or fidelity cost is currently unattractive. The portfolio does not imply that all fifteen should be implemented.

For the stock robot, we reuse the existing [validated CPU infrastructure](../robocurve-acceptance/preflight/README.md), with315 convex collision hulls per arm, actual fingers, palm, wrist and upstream links. A94mm nominal aperture and a reachable tip never count as an acquired grasp or a swept path. No unexecuted test is described as passed. Tool wrench capacity, second-arm interaction and final task contacts remain separate gates.

## Scoring and investment discipline

Every finalist has a released mechanical outcome, an external inspection/function test, reset procedure and at least three incorrect solutions. Numerical acceptance tolerances proposed here are benchmark design choices unless explicitly attributed to the manufacturer. Hidden simulator state may implement an observable measurement; hidden flags or scripted attachment may not replace the causal mechanism.

Only one first investment is selected. Source inspection and inexpensive numerical geometry extraction are distinguished from a contact prototype. No GPU, paid service, procurement, native evaluation, official submission or unrelated task modification was performed.
