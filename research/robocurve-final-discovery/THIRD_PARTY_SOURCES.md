# Third-party evidence and asset use

This directory contains research evidence, not a submitted benchmark. No competing task implementation was executed or used to author a replacement task. Copied definitions/scoring files support attributed semantic comparison only.

- Toyota YUBI hardware/source is pinned to`dd8bd13d2fd8e5003057243576f88be333d95fc5`, CERN-OHL-W v2. Its source license is preserved under`agents/robot_build/evidence/yubi/LICENSE`; original fetch URLs and hashes accompany derived occurrence/feature measurements. No original hardware geometry was modified by this research.
- Faze4 is pinned to`a482f3993457fd5b4071d740f0bc826ffc0d7b32`, with its published CERN-OHL-S v2 license retained in its evidence folder. Native benchmark asset reuse requires complying with that license, not merely citing this report.
- Yale OpenHand F3 publishes CC-BY-NC3.0 at the inspected head. This research does not assert that noncommercial asset terms automatically permit every eventual competition use; that must be checked before implementation.
- Create3, ODRI, OpenMV, CELLO/GELLO, Opentrons and other publisher evidence retain repository/pin/path provenance and available license notices. A source file being public does not transfer ownership.
- Household manuals were read as original branded PDFs from the explicitly named RealAppliance mirror. Their SHA256/pins are retained; live OEM provenance was not independently authenticated. Product manuals/figures remain their authors' materials. Selected excerpts/figures are retained for the requested research analysis; no permission to redistribute every appliance scan or reuse a proprietary CAD model is inferred.

Source inspection, derived geometric measurements and normal engineering approximation are distinct from permission to distribute a future task. The next implementation plan includes checking exact asset permissions before native packaging.
