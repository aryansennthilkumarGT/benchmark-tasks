## PRIORITIZE HIGH BUILDABILITY

We have lost too much time investigating physically interesting tasks that require inaccessible hardware data or complex unvalidated material simulation.

Strongly prefer candidates with:

- Actual published procedures.
- Accessible source documentation.
- Known dimensions or CAD.
- Genuine, tractable mechanical interfaces.
- Stock YAM-compatible manipulation.
- Limited numbers of crucial contact surfaces.
- Clearly observable retained/completed states.
- Manageable implementation cost.
- Genuine bimanual or long-horizon manipulation complexity.
- A realistic route to a full-score scripted demonstration.

Rigid or moderately compliant mechanical mechanisms may be especially attractive.

Flexible or deformable objects are allowed when the essential physics can be faithfully modeled with a defensible approximation.

Do NOT require perfect OEM CAD or exhaustive material testing for every candidate. The rubric permits disclosed, faithful simplifications.

But never replace a critical real mechanism with an arbitrary scripted latch, invisible attachment, proximity trigger, rigid substitute or fake physics.

Distinguish normal engineering approximations from changes that erase the skill.

## STOCK YAM FEASIBILITY

Use our existing validated YAM geometry and CPU preflight infrastructure.

Remember:

- There are 315 collision hulls per arm.
- Finger-tip reach alone does not prove grasp feasibility.
- Palm, wrist, elbow, second-arm and tool clearance matter.
- Nominal gripper aperture does not prove stable acquisition.
- Gripper force settings do not automatically prove tool wrench capacity.
- A source-derived robot reach bound is necessary, not sufficient.

For each finalist, screen:

- Required object scale and mass.
- Plausible grasp points.
- Tool acquisition if applicable.
- Bimanual coordination.
- Support or fixture reaction.
- Critical insertion/removal paths.
- Major collision risks.
- Whether normal YAM hands can perform the task without changing the hardware.

For top candidates with enough genuine geometry, perform actual CPU reach/collision tests and preserve outputs.

For candidates without a complete path, distinguish PLANNING PLAUSIBILITY from VERIFIED CLEARANCE.

Do not claim an unexecuted test passed.

- How to reset the hardware.
- At least three plausible incorrect solutions.
