# Occupation, equipment and novelty limits

O*NET 49-3023.00, Automotive Service Technicians and Mechanics, establishes vehicle inspection/repair as an identifiable occupation. LuK's manufacturer repair manual names actual single-plate Audi A4/A5/Q5/A6 0B1/0B2/0B3 applications and SAC tool 400 0237 10, with the step-by-step installation-tool sequence in section 10.6. Incorrect SAC mounting/preload can disturb the adjuster and require repair; this benchmark evaluates only the unloaded carrier preparation, not prevention of all installation damage. Sealey VS011A.V3 corroborates the independent three-slot carrier/retainer setup in its manufacturer instructions. URLs and retrieved content hashes are in SOURCES.json.

This is substantively different from the rejected generic spring-loaded plate: an occupied central bore and three 120° axes constrain a real slotted tool carrier; three independent contact-threaded nuts must secure it flush before any pressure operation. There is no fictional spring preload score. It also differs from the preserved shackle task: the carrier must fit three axes at once and clear a separate central mandrel, then accumulate three distinct retainers while resting on shoulder supports. Both share generic threading/regrasp mechanics; that overlap is disclosed rather than a universal novelty assertion.

Known named-suite comparisons are limited: common LIBERO/RoboCasa/BEHAVIOR household interactions do not establish industrial SAC carrier mounting, while generic screw/nut assembly tasks share individual mechanics. An industrial assembly comparison must evaluate full setup topology and occupational step, not rely on names/colors.
