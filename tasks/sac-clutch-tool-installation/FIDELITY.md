# Fidelity and procedure boundary

LuK, *Repair Solution for Clutch Modules* (September 2012), section 10.6 pp27–30 describes an actual automotive service procedure. Disc centering and cover dowel location precede three temporary studs at 120°. The three-arm spindle carrier and pressure piece fit over them; three independent knurled nuts secure it flush with the studs before the central spindle is turned to pretension the diaphragm. Permanent fastening and later tool removal are subsequent operations. The Sealey VS011A.V3 fitting sequence independently corroborates the carrier/retainer mounting before pressure-screw compression, without proving dimensional interchangeability.

This benchmark stops at that unloaded carrier/retainer setup. Upstream centering, cover positioning and stud installation are fixed prepared conditions. A 15 mm authored centering mandrel physically occupies the central clearance path. Three contact-bearing stud shoulders support the carrier. The carrier has actual open radial slots and a central bore, plus a hollow, retracted spindle envelope. Three independent retainers must pass over separate upper studs, follow contact threads and bear against the carrier. Collision geometry includes both male and female helices and bearing faces. No software joint automatically engages a thread.

The original benchmark fixture dimensions, masses, material choices and tolerances are **benchmark-authored approximations**, explicitly authorized for this CPU phase. The three stud count and 120° spacing are manufacturer-documented. The manufacturer's flywheel-side thread availability is recorded separately; the chosen prepared lower M8×1.25 interface does not imply any upper thread dimension. The custom upper 12 mm-major / 3 mm-pitch square thread is an authored geometry that can be reproduced, not an ISO M12 or LuK measurement claim. There is no independently measured parameter in this package.

`PARAMETER_TABLE.md`, `parameters.csv` and `fixture-profile.json` classify the complete geometry. Derived dimensions include the 2 mm slot diametral clearance, 4 mm hollow-spindle/mandrel clearance, thread relief, absolute flush/phase datums and mass-matched bounding-box inertia. Paint witness marks identify retainer rotation. All nominal poses and collider dimensions are exported in metres, mass in kg and inertia in kg·m².

The prepared surrogate is rigid. Disc splines, DMF internals, diaphragm compliance, self-adjuster state, pressure-piece loading and permanent bolts are not manipulated or evaluated. Their installation and physics are outside the selected step. The integral retracted spindle envelope is a no-contact clearance object, not a functional pressure actuator. Three elevated authored shoulders replace the detailed cover/tool support interface for unloaded mounting only. No preload, clamp force, fastening torque, clutch operation, installation certification or interchangeable real tool is claimed. In particular LuK's application-specific 22 Nm + 90° permanent bolt instruction is not applied to these retainers or used as a spring force law.

The represented skill is placing one three-slot body over three distinct axes and an occupied central bore, acquiring small independent retainers, starting compatible threads, repeatedly rotating/regrasping while maintaining axial descent, and leaving a seated assembly unsupported by grippers. Authored coarse threads reduce dexterity demands relative to fine production threads. This task cannot establish proficiency at complete SAC installation, avoiding self-adjuster damage or controlling diaphragm preload.

All parts are rigid steel approximations, with authored friction and mass/inertia. Threads use square-profile faceted contact prisms rather than production triangular flanks, material deformation or thread stress. Native testing must establish that solver resolution/contact offsets support real engagement and retention, and that ordinary YAM grippers can acquire/regrasp the knurled rim without slip. Numerical checks and ideal tracking do not resolve those risks. If actual contacts fail, revise geometry/controller and regenerate all affected evidence before evaluation.
