# Setup, manufacturing and reset

The table is z=0.7475 m in the pinned YAM station; +z is up, units are metres, quaternions xyzw. `scene.json` lists every initial pose, primitive, body mass, COM and diagonal inertia. `parameters.csv` enumerates all collider dimensions. All seven USD files are original exports from `generate_scene.py`, not external meshes.

The prepared fixture is a 216 mm diameter, 40 mm high rigid base at (0.24,0). A visible prepared disc/cover ring, three locating dowels and a centering mandrel are fixed initial conditions. Three stud axes lie on the 62 mm circle at 0/120/240°. Shoulder tops are z=0.8175 m. Stud tips are z=0.8375 m. Carrier plate thickness is 8 mm, so its seated origin is z=0.8215 m. Seated retainer bottoms are z=0.8255 m and tops are z=0.8375 m. Entry nut origins are z=0.8435 m; four clockwise turns at 3 mm/turn descend 12 mm.

The carrier starts at (0.22,0.205,0.8215), on three passive raised supports, with three identical handling tabs and its retracted hollow central spindle. Each retainer starts separately on a 45 mm-high pedestal, center z=0.7985: (0.12,-0.18), (0.21,-0.20), (0.30,-0.18). Paint a radial witness line on the upper rim of each retainer. Do not lubricate or add magnetic retention without changing the documented contact model and requalification.

For a physical benchmark reproduction, machine the base/support shoulders and slots from the exact CAD construction coordinates in `scene.json`; the USD primitive export can be converted into a solid CAD model by unioning each body's positive shapes. Preserve the open bores and slots: never fill the central bore or use convex hulls of entire compound parts. Manufacture the male square-thread ridge and complementary female helical ridge with the same handedness, 3 mm lead and half-pitch phase offset; `generate_scene.helix` gives every segment centre and cross section. The intended machinable equivalent is a smooth square helix at the same root/crest radii and 0.20 mm flank relief. Replacing faceted simulation flanks with smooth machining is a disclosed approximation requiring its own geometry/retention check. Use steel stock; round sharp nonmating edges without changing bearing/slot clearances. Use ±0.05 mm mating-dimension tolerance and adjust ballast away from mating surfaces to the stated masses (±5 g). The base and two trays must be rigidly bolted to the bench. Do not use this authored fixture for vehicle repair or loaded clutch compression.

Build lower prepared fixture sockets independently to M8×1.25 only if reproducing that authored lower connection. The upper contact thread is a separate custom 12 mm/3 mm square design, **not** inferred from that lower socket. Document the actual lower stud anchor and adequate depth when making hardware; its installation is not scored. A hardware build needs measured actual contact/friction and dimensional evidence before comparison with simulation; this package provides design specifications, not an assertion that hardware exists.

Reset all seven assembly roots to their exact initial translations/identity quaternions and set dynamic linear/angular velocity to zero. Anchors remain fixed. Clear the scorer's histories, failure latch and hold timer; clear the controller's targets/recovery/events. Use seed 0 and zero object jitter. Repeat each native case twice after independent resets and reject >3 mm reset drift. No consumable or pressure-spring state is involved. The pressure spindle remains rigidly retracted through the entire trial.
