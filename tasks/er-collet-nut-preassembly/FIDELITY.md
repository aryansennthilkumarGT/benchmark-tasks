# Fidelity and construct boundary

REGO-FIX's dimensioned ER sheet, Drawing4, gives the 8 mm ER32 profile: D1 33 mm, D2 32 mm, D3 29.2 mm, total length 40 mm, groove L2 3.6 mm, nose L3 5.5 mm, 8° body and 30° nose tapers. The source drawing was inspected, not interpreted from a product photograph. The standard matching nut/cavity family is M40×1.5, but holder threading is outside this task.

MariTool's manufacturer ER FAQ explicitly explains that the outside groove snaps into the nut's eccentric ring, tilting helps engagement, and the eccentricity permits insertion. It also says the cam is **not guaranteed** to hold an **unsupported** collet; the assembly may need angling during installation to avoid the collet falling out. Accordingly this task evaluates a supported preassembly, not hanging retention, pull-out strength or a completed tool clamp. REGO-FIX's actual ER product catalogue and precision-tooling article establish the equipment context; its located video was not used as an unverified transcript. Cross-manufacturer sources document the common ER interface, not exact interoperability of this authored nut.

The collet's declared nominal profile dimensions are manufacturer-documented. Through-bore, annular groove and nose are physical collision geometry. Axial stacks and faceted rings approximate the tapers. Slots and elastic collapse are omitted for unloaded geometric preassembly; this rigid approximation prevents compressing the collet through the cam as a shortcut and differs from some real slotted-collet behavior. It is not a spring-steel stress, runout or clamping simulation.

Every cam/nut/clamp/support dimension and physical parameter is benchmark-authored or derived, not measured REGO-FIX hardware. The cam has an 18.5 mm opening radius offset +2.9 mm X, 0.6 mm thickness, a 19 mm front bore and 20.2 mm upper cavity. Those values were engineered to allow a tilted nose passage, side engagement and rocking path while blocking a straight axial insertion. The collet is lowered with a +2.5 mm X offset at −0.25 rad tilt, translated to engage the negative-X groove edge, then rocked about an authored groove-side pivot. These angles, clearances and waypoints are fixture engineering choices, not manufacturer procedural dimensions or forces.

The surrogate nut is bench-clamped and three lower pads support the collet's nose. This replaces hand stabilization and ensures the final state does not depend on unsupported cam retention. No cutting tool, spindle, holder threads, tightening torque, collet compression, balance, TIR or cutting load is evaluated. No magnetic/success-triggered attachment or constraint connects the collet and nut.

The 150 g collet mass, fixed fixture/cradle masses, steel-like friction and bounding-box inertia are authored assumptions, not measurements. True jaw purchase on the tapered surface, cam contact behavior, solver resolution, reset and two real expert demonstrations still require native validation. CPU geometry and ideal rigid-grasp tracking cannot certify them. See SOURCES.json, PARAMETERS.md, parameters.csv and CONSTRUCT_VALIDITY.md.
