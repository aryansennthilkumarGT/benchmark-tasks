# Score and physical observation

The scorer reads actual body poses/velocities, rail joint, robot clearance and independent collision telemetry. It never reads expert phase names. A first frame must show the prepared drive within 3 mm of its initial position. Subsequent displacement above 2 m/s times the sample interval plus 10 mm is rejected as an unobserved state replacement. This broad bound is an integrity check, not an intended robot speed.

A correctly observed side-rail opening of at least 4.5 mm while the drive is seated earns 0.25. Current extraction earns 0.5 when the drive origin is at least 55 mm above its initial origin or more than 125 mm away horizontally. Rail return within 0.5 mm adds 0.25; correct staging adds 0.25, within 1.5 mm laterally, 0.5 mm vertically and 0.025 rad of level. Full score requires two seconds below 8 mm/s and 0.15 rad/s, with robot geometry at least 20 mm from dynamic parts. Otherwise credit is capped at 0.95. Removing or disturbing the staged drive removes that credit.

The physical opposed pins and bores enforce release and disengagement; there is no additional requirement to reproduce the expert's exact tilt height, hand choice, intermediate translation or phase timing. A collision-free alternative counts. Straight lifting while pins remain engaged is independently tested to intersect real geometry. Collision telemetry comes from the native collector, never from the policy.

Rail body/joint agreement must be within 0.4 mm and 0.025 rad. Anchor movement above 1 mm/0.025 rad, rail travel outside [-0.2,7.2] mm, body loss below the stated loss plane, penetration above 0.35 mm, malformed/nonfinite telemetry or conflicting timestamps latch zero. Observation gaps reset the stability timer. Identical repeated frames are idempotent.

Physical judging uses fixed reference scales and calibrated video for rail displacement, extraction, staging, clearance and stability. Calipers/height gauges establish initial geometry before running. A view must resolve the 0.5 mm vertical threshold with at most 0.1 mm uncertainty. No force, spring stress, exact Dell fit or electrical function is inferred from pose. No physical judging trial has been run.
