# Fixture reproduction and reset

Units are metres/kg/radians, quaternions xyzw, +Y is the moving side. The standard table top is z=0.7475. Bracket base origin is (0.24,0,0.7975), drive origin (0.24,0,0.8039). The drive's 100×69.85×6.8 mm outer envelope is manufacturer-dimensional; all mounting interfaces are authored. The drive sits on 3 mm support pads. Two pins per side lie at x=±30 mm, y=±35 mm and z=6.4 mm relative to the bracket origin, axis Y. Each pin is 3 mm diameter, 6 mm long, penetrating the case roughly 2.925 mm; each case-side bore has 4.8 mm diameter and 6 mm depth.

The +Y rail translates 6 mm for normal release, hard-limited to 7 mm. Its authored handling extension reaches y=130 mm, leaving the robot arms clear of the case. The opposite rail stays fixed. A 0.25 rad tilt around the opposite side's X axis raises the freed edge, then +4.5 mm translation clears the remaining pins. The full motion is generated in ten small tilt increments with common-fraction translation/rotation control to preserve the pivot path.

Staging tray origin is (0.25,-0.19,0.7475), four post tops z=0.7725, target drive origin z=0.7759. `scene.json` gives all local shapes, initial transforms, joints, contact coefficients, masses, COM and inertia. `parameters.csv` enumerates every collider dimension and provenance category. `generate_scene.py` is the original geometric construction recipe; USD primitives are the original exported assets.

For physical fixture reproduction, machine a hollow drive-case surrogate to the documented outside envelope and authored open bores; trim/ballast its internal noncontact volume to 45 g. Do not damage a functioning SSD to create benchmark bores. Assemble the bracket on a rigidly anchored bench base with a linear-guide side rail and a 180 N/m return spring. Use the stated pin/bores, 7 mm stops and handling extension; a real plastic bracket is not guaranteed to have this stiffness or geometry. Specify ±0.05 mm mating tolerance, verify all pins clear at 6 mm opening, and measure actual guide friction/damping before comparing real results. Smooth noncontact edges, preserving retention surfaces. The remaining internal case void is not simulated; it must not change external mass/COM or pin contacts. Never claim production interchangeability.

Restore bracket and tray anchors, drive pose/zero velocities, rail joint position and velocity zero, and the free-rail body implied by that joint. Clear scorer failure/release/tilt/withdrawal/hold state and controller/recovery. Seed 0 and no jitter. Native tests independently reset each case twice and reject reset drift. No consumables, electrical power or software installation are involved.
