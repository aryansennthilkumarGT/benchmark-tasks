# Occupational importance

Computer user support specialists (SOC 15-1232) perform hardware installation and minor repair as explicit O*NET core work. BLS reports 750,600 workers in 2025. Dell's actual removed-bracket drive replacement step grounds this instance of that work. The count describes the occupation, not a claim that every worker services this computer or removes drives daily. It excludes network-support specialists and avoids inflating the claim with all IT employment. Unplugged, removed assemblies are a normal service boundary; no electrical or diagnostic work is claimed.

Primary URLs, retrieved content hashes, quoted duties, disjoint SOC codes and counts are in occupation-evidence.json. Counts are source observations, not independently measured hardware evidence. Manufacturer procedures and dimensional provenance remain in SOURCES.json and the fidelity/setup documents.
