# Physical scoring measurements

These are reproduction instructions, not measurements from a built fixture. All tolerances are benchmark-authored. Use the original inert mock-up; no powered or loaded nailer is required.

Calibrate two orthogonal close-up cameras against a steel rule or gauge block at the service plane. Measure the door angle about its actual hinge;1rad opening earns the first milestone. Inspect every brad solid, not only its center: all must be5mm clear of the nose's stated bounding region. In the tray, every vertex must lie inside x±37.5mm/y±42.5mm, at heights1.8–19mm above its datum. Calipers and photographs can establish these static tests.

For secured closure, inspect both crosspin ends beneath the actual arm lips, using the dimensions and planes in PARAMETERS.md/scene.json and an end-on photograph. Door seating is within0.005rad; a100mm witness therefore permits approximately0.5mm transverse change. Measure lever angle -0.06..0.01rad against the closed stop. A dial indicator on the sliding draw cartridge measures at least0.1mm extension relative to its independently established unloaded zero. Prepared preload is not the unloaded zero.

The past-center check is geometric rather than a hidden force target. From a calibrated image locate lever pivot O, eccentric E and pin P. With k the hinge-axis unit vector, compute dot((E-P)/|E-P|, cross(k,E-O)); require at most-0.5mm/rad. This is the signed draw-length derivative, indicating that tension drives the lever toward its closed stop. A printed coordinate worksheet and ruler/calipers suffice for the computation; no claimed OEM torque is involved. Actual retention is separately tested after releasing the fixture's manipulation inputs.

For full score record at least2s continuously at20Hz. Every pair of samples must differ by at most0.5mm at all stated door/lever/pin/brad witnesses; a single photograph is insufficient. Use calibrated close-up video or dial indicators with substantially finer resolution than0.5mm. Confirm both grippers and robot links are at least20mm from the task solids. A fresh episode must begin in the explicitly prepared jammed/preloaded state; substituting a finished setup is invalid.

Simulator integrity checks are continuous consistency guards: joint limits, fixed-body displacement, disconnected parts, lost brad and excess solid penetration permanently invalidate a run. On hardware inspect intact joints, stops, fixtures and fastener, and stop at damage/deformation. Numerical200um solid-overlap and500um articulation-consistency tolerances are solver guards; they are not claimed to be directly observable OEM damage thresholds. Hardware validation would need actual fixture/gripper measurement and uncertainty reporting. No physical trial has been performed.
