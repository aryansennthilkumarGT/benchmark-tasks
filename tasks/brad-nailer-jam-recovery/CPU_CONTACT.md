# CPU evidence and collision tooling

MuJoCo3.3.7 is a supplemental mechanism engine, not Isaac/YAM or physical hardware. Every passive joint, contact solid, mass and principal inertia comes from scene.json. All four parent-child contact pairs remain enabled. The capped diagnostic drives and pin/brad wrenches are disclosed test manipulators; no object pose or task joint is written after stepping starts. The authored input forces are not manufacturer measurements or robot capacity estimates.

The sequential probe opens the actual mechanism, extracts the brad clear of the swept door, closes the door while carrying the pin, guides the pin through the hook mouths and seats it while the unactuated lever follows. Transitions after the opening/carry stages use measured mouth-pose settling and pin capture. Pin and door guidance are removed in sequence before a separate lever push and released opening-torque check. Exact event times are logged. This validates the contact mechanism under those inputs, not frictional robot grasps or20Hz YAM control. Current-source logs retain failed earlier strategies rather than treating old evidence as qualification of changed geometry.

Native and supplemental timesteps are1/1920s at20Hz robot control. Supplemental contact uses the documented elliptic/Newton settings in cpu_contact.py; margin=gap preserves zero active rest offset. Physics materials and numerical contact settings are authored. Restitution is zero. The penetration integrity limit remains0.20mm.

Offline FK uses pinned YAM URDF collision vertices. SciPy1.16.3 ConvexHull removes redundant interior tessellation without changing the convex point set: tests verify every original point lies inside the hull and every retained vertex is an original point. This is not mesh decimation or a bounding-box substitution. Task/robot checks use SAT. Dense inter-arm checks use CPU python-fcl0.7.0.8/libccd GJK/EPA, tested against analytic boxes and complete SAT on genuine convex cases. A reproduced libccd degeneracy overestimates the penetration of exactly coincident convex transforms; those cases explicitly fall back to complete SAT. Failed tooling tests were repaired, not relabeled as passes. Collision sampling still does not establish swept-volume safety or native contact behavior.

The ideal fixture assumes rigid grasps, quasistatic passive-link solutions and brad settling. The pin/lever solution projects the spring-rest eccentric onto its actual circle, checks joint limits and reports the resulting grasp residual; it does not directly assign phases as physical success. Released support and grasp friction remain assumptions. The replay auditor separately checks task-solid penetration, both arms' actual FK hulls, table clearance, inter-arm contact and the scorer with conservative per-link release boxes. Full source input hashes prevent traces from qualifying changed code/assets.
