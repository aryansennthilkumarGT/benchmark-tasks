# Reproduction

The entire fixture is authored in SI units in generate_scene.py, dimensions.py and scene.json. PARAMETERS.md and parameter-provenance.json classify every numeric scene value. Build the original three USDs with `python generate_scene.py`; verify them with `python verify_assets.py`. The result must reproduce the tracked assets exactly under the pinned usd-core stack. Do not silently substitute manufacturer CAD or treat these dimensions as measured DCN680 geometry.

For a physical research fixture, machine the nose trough, narrowed rear backplate and front shoulders from steel or aluminium to the explicit primitive coordinates. Machine the door and separate lever; use the stated hinge shafts, open bores and clearances. Form the two-arm draw member with an articulated compliant cartridge matching the authored20kN/m effective axial spring and travel, rather than assuming wire stock automatically reproduces that law. Produce both fixed capture arms and the52mm crosspin. Check both pin-end engagements, closed door clearance and the sign of the eccentric's length derivative before use. The table gives target masses; ballast or redistribute components to reproduce the disclosed COM and full inertia, and record genuine measurements separately. Fixture tolerances must preserve the0.1–0.2mm radial running clearances; measure actual clearances and report deviations. The5µm contact initialization is a simulation preparation, not a machining overlap.

The contextual case and empty magazine are original envelopes. The bench base, supports and two clamps must physically restrain an unpowered fixture. Do not place a functional loaded nailer into a robot workspace. A physical experiment would use an inert mock-up, the documented rigid bent fastener and no firing energy. Ordinary workshop risk assessment and real gripper/payload measurements are required before physical use; no hardware validation is claimed here.

Reset seed0 with no pose jitter. Restore every rigid root pose and passive joint from scene.json, zero velocities and independently restore spring drive equilibrium to zero. The prepared0.5mm draw preload is not the spring rest target. Clear scorer history, robot controller and trace reader state. Run independent resets before each native trial. Contact pairs remain enabled throughout.
