# Reproducible unloaded bench setup

All coordinates are metres in the pinned YAM station frame; Z is up. Table top is 0.7475 m. Gravity is (0,0,-9.81) m/s²; physics step is 1/480 s, control is 20 Hz, seed 0 and object_jitter=0. No task mechanism motor or virtual attachment is used. The real parts are rigid bodies with contact threads, so the task has no fictitious revolute/prismatic spring joint. Fixed assets are bench supports anchored through explicit USD fixed joints.

Reference envelope: Green Pin G-4161, GPGHBB16. Bow stock 16 mm; pin 19 mm; eye diameter 40 mm and axial thickness 16 mm; clear inside eye gap 27 mm; inside bow length 64 mm and width 43 mm; overall pin 89 mm. The manufacturer table gives a combined 0.63 kg. The model assigns 0.45 kg to the bow and 0.18 kg to the pin; this split is an authored approximation. No rating is inferred from it.

Original faceted compound collision shapes are generated by generate_scene.py. The near eye has a 19.7 mm clear bore. The far eye has a 20.2 mm root bore and square-profile contact teeth at 3/4-10 UNC pitch (2.54 mm). They approximate the real triangular profile; the author-defined flank clearance is 0.14 mm, 64 sectors/turn. No success-dependent collision filtering, screw joint or snapping is used. The guide lug bore is 20.6 mm, 12 mm thick.

The remaining body shapes, scales, offsets, orientations and bounds are exhaustively specified in scene.json. Diagonal inertia uses a positive bounding-box equivalent, not a calibrated manufacturer tensor. All bodies use the original asset compiler material: static friction 0.65, dynamic friction 0.50, restitution 0.0. These are author-selected model coefficients. Native actor contact offset is 0.03 mm with zero rest offset. The runtime uses explicit solver settings and records actual observed states. Numerical stability and reset settling require the native tests.

The sling link is a closed rigid ring on a bench stand, already threaded through the open bow. The open shackle nest supports the far eye and lower bow without blocking the lug/pin path. It leaves the bow free to slide the 6 mm required for alignment. The separate pin cradle supports its collar and shaft; it does not attach the pin. All stands and their geometry/masses are authored bench fixtures, not a claimed manufacturer workstation. The left robot releases into the nest and parks before the right robot threads the pin.

Reset: restore the exact scene transforms, zero velocities, and fresh scorer/controller state. Settle through the pinned official reset; require the authored-reset native case to keep free pin and shackle within 3 mm. Inspect the actual native initial image for the ring-through-bow topology, unblocked lug bore and pin in its cradle. Repeated deterministic robot poses alone do not establish repeatable physical reset.

For a physical trial, use unmodified matched hardware and fabricated supports at the stated layout. Do not machine or alter rated shackles to match the simulation approximation. The procedure is unloaded; actual operational rigging requires independent selection, inspection and manufacturer securing steps. See MEASUREMENT.md for physical judging and calibrated views.
