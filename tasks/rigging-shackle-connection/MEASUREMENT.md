# Physical judging without dismantling the result

Use an unmodified matching manufacturer shackle/pin, a fabricated lug/link support and the stated bench nest. The model follows nominal dimensions but is not a substitute for real approved lifting hardware. No load is lifted during this bench manipulation trial. A real operational lift requires the manufacturer's complete inspection, selection and securing procedure independently.

Before the trial, document the labelled matching pair, empty bore, starting pin in its cradle, open shackle/link setup and the ruler/fiducial layout. Use two fixed macro cameras at the collar and the opposite eye, plus an overhead view. Put a calibrated scale in each measured plane; require at least 50 pixels/mm and independently verify <=0.10 mm scale error. A camera view that cannot resolve the 0.35 mm collar boundary does not validate that boundary.

The alignment condition is measured from the shackle/lug bore centres and the authored fixture reference, using calibrated overhead views before pin insertion. Continue tracking the outer eye/lug references after insertion. Model coordinates are deterministic; the physical jig needs corresponding measured reference marks. Offset and tilt thresholds must not be inferred from colour or object names.

The pin crosses the near eye, lug bore and far eye. Confirm its emerging tip in the opposite-eye view; verify the shank/eye nominal spans and full-depth reference from the dimensioned part. The source uses a 59 mm shank and 59 mm outside eye span. Do not substitute a shorter pin or infer a connection merely from a collar near the top eye.

Measure the collar-to-eye gap in side views from opposite directions after release. Backlighting and fixed coplanar scales allow noncontact edge measurement without probing or disturbing the result. The largest observed gap must be <=0.35 mm, lateral offset <=0.8 mm and tilt <=0.025 rad. Record the full tightening sequence to count at least 5.8 advancing turns and distinguish it from free spinning. A small removable paint witness on the physical pin tab may aid counting; it changes no interface or score condition.

Require two seconds of no visible movement with both hands >=20 mm away. Calibrated physical measurements and camera coverage must themselves be demonstrated before claiming a successful physical trial. No such photographs or measurements have yet been produced. Rated strength, calibrated wrench torque and deformation are not obtainable from these observations and are not scored.
