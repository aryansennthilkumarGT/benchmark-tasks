# Occupational importance

Hand laborers and freight, stock, and material movers (SOC 53-7062) have an explicit O*NET task: attaching slings, hooks and other lifting devices. BLS projects 2,940,300 workers in that occupation in 2025. This is broader than the small specialist-rigger occupation; the core load-attachment task provides the connection, rather than counting unrelated workers. It does not establish how many use this shackle size or this bench aid. Green Pin separately identifies frequent assembly/disassembly as the screw-pin product's intended use. The task isolates joining selected hardware before any lift; inspection, load selection and strength certification are excluded.

Primary URLs, retrieved content hashes, quoted duties, disjoint SOC codes and counts are in occupation-evidence.json. Counts are source observations, not independently measured hardware evidence. Manufacturer procedures and dimensional provenance remain in SOURCES.json and the fidelity/setup documents.
