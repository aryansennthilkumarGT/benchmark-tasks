The candidate exchanges an SDS-plus construction chisel through an automatic radial ball lock with independent spring sleeve release. The closed slot retains the shank while deliberately permitting axial play; separate open grooves transmit torque. Removing a retained tool needs sleeve retraction while controlling the tool, and replacing it needs keyed alignment and actual ball/cam displacement. Tool identity/count and occupational wording alone are not claimed as novelty.

The accepted bayonet-mate task pushes a lugged cap below a spring-supported roof and turns it90deg against stops. Its locking rotation is after insertion. The radial ball and independently translated sleeve retain/release the tool, and free axial travel remains valid after locking. Replacing this mechanism with a fixed-distance peg or generic bayonet would invalidate the distinction.
