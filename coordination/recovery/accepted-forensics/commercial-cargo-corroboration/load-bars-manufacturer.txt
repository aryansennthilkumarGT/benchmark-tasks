Load Bars for Trucks and Trailers
Shopify API |
May 30, 2013
If installing E-track and shoring beams is not option in your trailer or truck, cargo load bars are another great choice. Load bars or cargo bars are perfect for sectioning space in a trailer or truck that doesn't have E-track.
Designed in a heavy duty metal, rubber pads on each end grip the wall to keep the bar in place.
How To Secure Your Load with a Cargo Bar
Watch the video below to learn how to install these trucking essentials:
Choosing a Cargo Bar
Two sizes are available depending on your application. Both have bolt-on padded feet for no-slip traction.
The
light-duty load bar
is adjustable from 40-60" and allows you to slowly adjust the tension for the perfect fit.
The
commercial-grade load bar
adjusts from 89.75-104.5" and locks in place for fast and secure placement.
Cargo Bar Accessories
Accessories include
replacement cargo bar feet
, a r
eplacement tension lock body
, and a
cargo bar holder
easily installs on the wall of the trailer or van. It holds two load bars securely in place when not in use.
